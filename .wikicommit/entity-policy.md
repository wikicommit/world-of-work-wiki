Pages are about work, conditions, measures, surveys and the organisations that study them —
not about individual people. Do not create a page for a report's author, a researcher, an
official or a spokesperson; mention their contribution in the body where the source does.

Do not create pages about individual workers named in case descriptions (a death from
overwork, an injury, a court case), whether or not they are living. Their cases may be
described in the body of a page about the condition, without the name unless the source's
own public summary uses it.

Do not over-apply this: historical figures who shaped how work is understood (for example
the founders of occupational medicine or of time-use research) may have pages when a source
treats them as its subject.
