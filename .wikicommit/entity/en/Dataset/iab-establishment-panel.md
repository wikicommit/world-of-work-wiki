The IAB Establishment Panel (German: IAB-Betriebspanel) is a representative survey of employers on the determinants of economic activity at establishment level. Around 15,000 establishments of all sizes and industries are surveyed every year; the population is establishments with at least one employee subject to social insurance contributions. As a comprehensive longitudinal dataset it is the basis for research on the demand side of the labour market.

## Details

The survey has run since 1993 in the western and since 1996 in the eastern German federal states. Establishments were first asked about the number of men and women in management positions in 2004; the question was asked every four years at first and every two years since 2012. A focus module on measures to reconcile family and work was first fielded in 2002 and for the sixth time in 2024; in 2024 it covered family-friendly working hours, contact during parental leave, childcare support, support for employees caring for relatives and leave for fathers, the last of which was not asked about in 2016. The Institut für Arbeitsmarkt- und Berufsforschung (IAB) uses the panel to document the [[DefinedTerm/gender-leadership-gap]], as in [[Report/women-hold-less-than-a-third-of-top-management-positions]].
