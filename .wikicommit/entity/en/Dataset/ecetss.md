The ECETSS (Encuesta Nacional a trabajadores sobre Condiciones de Empleo, Trabajo, Salud y Seguridad) is Argentina's national survey of workers on their employment, working, health and safety conditions. Its 2018 edition was promoted by the Superintendencia de Riesgos del Trabajo (SRT) through its Observatory of Occupational Safety and Health, created in September 2017, and was designed, planned and implemented jointly with the directorate for macroeconomic studies and labour statistics (DGEMyEL) of the Secretaría de Gobierno de Trabajo y Empleo — the SRT and that secretariat both being under the Ministry of Production and Labour — with the support of the Ibero-American Social Security Organization (OISS). Its aim was to obtain data complementing information systems on employment, social security and occupational safety and health — characterising the conditions of the whole employed population, including workers not covered by the occupational risk system, identifying priority areas for action, designing and monitoring preventive programmes, and allowing international comparison.

## Details

The 2018 survey is described as the second national survey of its kind, after a first national survey in 2009, within a series of employment, working and health surveys run in Argentina since 2005 (including surveys of agricultural workers in 2013-2014 and deep-sea fishing workers in 2015). Compared with 2009 it broadened its scope from a survey of workers in establishments to one of all employed people interviewed in their homes, so that all segments of employment — registered and unregistered employees, domestic workers in private homes, own-account workers and employers — are covered; only unpaid family workers were excluded. Comparability with the 2009 questionnaire was maintained.

The target population was employed people aged 15 or over (anyone who worked at least one hour in the reference week, paid or not) living in urban localities of 2,000 or more inhabitants, an estimated universe of 16 million workers, with estimates for the national urban total and six regions (Greater Buenos Aires, Centre, Cuyo, Northeast, Northwest and Patagonia). The sample was drawn from the 2010 national census frame in four stages — localities, census tracts, dwellings, and one randomly selected worker per household — and yielded 12,605 dwellings and 8,966 workers interviewed in 136 localities. Fieldwork ran from August to December 2018, with an effective response rate of 68.7% of eligible dwellings.

The questionnaire contains 362 questions grouped in 18 blocks, with separate modules for employees and for independent workers. Besides earlier national questionnaires, it drew on the [[Dataset/european-working-conditions-survey]], the Argentine adaptation of the Copenhagen Psychosocial Questionnaire (COPSOQ) for psychosocial risks, and the basic questionnaire for working conditions, employment and health surveys in Latin America and the Caribbean (CTESLAC); well-being was measured with the WHO-5 index. Informality among employees is measured in the same way as in Argentina's Permanent Household Survey, and among independent workers by a method used in earlier surveys, as described under [[DefinedTerm/informal-employment]].

The results are presented in [[Report/ecetss-2018]].
