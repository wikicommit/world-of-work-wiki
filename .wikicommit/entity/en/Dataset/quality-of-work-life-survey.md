The Quality of Work Life Survey is a survey by Statistics Finland covering wage and salary earners aged 18 to 67. Its results are published under Statistics Finland's "Quality of work life" statistics, and a StatFin database table on persons who have done or been interested in remote work is updated irregularly. The review [[Report/remote-work-of-wage-and-salary-earners-in-finland-2023]] uses the 2023 round to describe remote work in Finland.

## Details

The survey's measurement series on remote work begins in 1997, when only a few per cent of wage and salary earners worked remotely. Until 2018 it was carried out as face-to-face interviews; the 2021 survey on the impact of COVID-19 on working conditions and the 2023 survey were web inquiries, so Statistics Finland cautions that the latest results are not fully comparable with 2018 and earlier. Results are broken down by sex, socio-economic group (upper-level and lower-level salaried employees and workers) and employer sector (central government, universities, private sector, local government, and wellbeing services counties and joint county authorities). There is no earlier comparison data on the share of working hours spent in remote work, so changes over time are inferred from respondents' main place of work.
