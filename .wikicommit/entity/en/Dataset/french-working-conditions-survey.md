The Working Conditions survey (*enquête Conditions de travail*) is carried out by Dares in collaboration with Insee. Since 1978, through its module on the organisation of working time, it has been — according to the Insee Références study [[Report/atypical-working-hours-women-less-qualified-and-foreign-born-most-exposed]] — the most reliable source of information on the organisation and rhythms of work in France.

## Details

The individual component of the 2019 edition was collected from October 2018 to May 2019 among 27,862 people living in ordinary dwellings in France. Respondents describe the usual hours of their main job with one or two periods of work, giving the time at which they usually start and finish work (or their first period of work), and say how many Saturdays and how many Sundays a year they work at their workplace. The 2025 study converted the reported start and finish times into minute-by-minute sequences of work and non-work and grouped them, by sequence analysis, into six types of usual hours — standard, long, shifted early in the morning, in the evening or at night, and [[DefinedTerm/split-working-hours]] — and used the Saturday and Sunday questions to measure regular [[DefinedTerm/weekend-work]]; it restricted its analysis to employees living in metropolitan France.
