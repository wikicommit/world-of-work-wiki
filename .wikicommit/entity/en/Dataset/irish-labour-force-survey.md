The Labour Force Survey (LFS) is the household survey from which Ireland's Central Statistics Office (CSO) draws the official estimate of employment. It samples 32,500 households across the country every three months, and lets the CSO ask households detailed questions on specific aspects of the labour market — such as hours worked, type of employment, availability for work, or illness or disability status — that might not be readily available from other data sources.

## Details

The CSO combines the survey with administrative data gathered by other public service bodies and adopted for statistical purposes, to give a timely picture of the labour market and of trends over time. How the CSO uses this combined survey and administrative data to describe recent labour market trends is set out in [[Report/employment-trends-in-ireland-what-cso-data-tells-us]].
