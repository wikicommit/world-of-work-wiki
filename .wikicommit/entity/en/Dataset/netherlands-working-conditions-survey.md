The Netherlands Working Conditions Survey (Nationale Enquête Arbeidsomstandigheden, NEA) is conducted every year by Statistics Netherlands (CBS) together with TNO among employees aged 15 to 75. It is the main source for the working conditions of employees in CBS's annual overview of the Dutch labour market ([[Report/working-conditions-the-labour-market-in-figures-2025]]), where its results are presented alongside those of a similar survey among the self-employed, the Self-employed Labour Survey (Zelfstandigen Enquête Arbeid, ZEA), which CBS and TNO conduct every two years.

## Details

In the 2025 overview, the figures drawn from the NEA and the ZEA cover self-reported physical and mental strain — physically heavy work, exposure to vibration and loud noise, hours of screen work, repetitive movements and awkward postures, dangerous work, work pressure, emotionally demanding work and autonomy — together with discrimination and undesirable behaviour at work, work-related mental fatigue, the work-relatedness of sickness absence, work accidents in the previous twelve months and occupational diseases diagnosed by a doctor. They are broken down by sex, age group and industry, and for several indicators CBS shows annual or two-yearly series from 2015 onwards. Figures on sickness absence by industry come from a separate CBS source, its quarterly survey of sickness absence.
