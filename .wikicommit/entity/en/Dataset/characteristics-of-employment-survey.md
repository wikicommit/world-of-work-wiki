The Characteristics of Employment survey is one of the Australian Bureau of Statistics' (ABS) supplementary surveys to the Labour Force Survey, collected in August. It covers the weekly earnings of employees, casual workers, independent contractors, trade union membership, labour hire, job flexibility and job security. Its results are published in the ABS *Characteristics of Employment, Australia* release (previously catalogue number 6333.0), and statistics from it appear in the ABS releases on employee earnings and on working arrangements.

## Details

The August 2025 collection, released on 12 December 2025, counted 2.4 million casual employees (19% of all employees and 17% of all employed people) and 1.1 million independent contractors (7.6% of all employed), and found that 36% of employed people usually worked from home. Median weekly earnings in employees' main job were $1,425, up $26 (1.9%) since August 2024, and median hourly earnings $42.90, up $2.90. Its data downloads cover employee earnings back to 1975 and working arrangements — job flexibility and security, forms of employment, fixed-term contracts, [[DefinedTerm/casual-employment]] and the characteristics of digital platform workers. Microdata is released in TableBuilder and in ABS DataLab, as a supplementary file to the Longitudinal Labour Force microdata.

August 2025 was the last time the survey was conducted in its current form. As part of modernising the Labour Force Survey, the ABS is ending the collection of supplementary surveys in specific calendar months (February, May and August) and, from the second half of 2026, is replacing them with a redesigned labour supplementary survey asked each month of respondents in their last month in the Labour Force Survey. That survey will combine content from Characteristics of Employment; Participation, Job Search and Mobility; the Barriers and Incentives and Casual Experience surveys collected in the Multi-Purpose Household Survey; and the quarter-month Labour Force Survey content not moving to the monthly survey. Its data will be compiled and published on a calendar-year basis, and the ABS expects a similar range of data to remain available.
