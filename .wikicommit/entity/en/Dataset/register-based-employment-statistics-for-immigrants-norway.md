Sysselsetting blant innvandrere, registerbasert (register-based employment statistics for immigrants) is a statistic published annually by Statistics Norway (SSB). It covers both employees and self-employed people aged 15–74 who are registered as resident in Norway in the National Population Register, and it underlies SSB's analyses of how much immigrants work, such as [[Report/how-much-do-immigrants-in-norway-work]], which looks at the 20–66 age group.

## Details

Immigrants are defined as people who have themselves immigrated to Norway, born abroad to foreign-born parents and with four foreign-born grandparents. The employed are people who did paid work of at least one hour in the reference week in November, together with people who have such work but were temporarily absent because of illness, holiday, paid leave or similar. Working full time means a combined position percentage of 100% or more across the main job and any secondary jobs; occupation is recorded for the main job using the Norwegian standard classification of occupations (STYRK08), and is not reported for the self-employed. For country background, SSB groups countries into "EU/EFTA etc." (the Nordic countries outside Norway, EU/EFTA, the UK, the USA, Canada, Australia and New Zealand) and "Africa, Asia etc." (Europe outside EU/EFTA and the UK, Africa, Asia, the Americas outside the USA and Canada, Oceania outside Australia and New Zealand, and polar regions).

The series has a break between 2014 and 2015, caused by the move to a new data source for employees (a-ordningen) and a new method for estimating self-employment, so figures before and after 2015 are not directly comparable and changes across the break must be interpreted with great caution.
