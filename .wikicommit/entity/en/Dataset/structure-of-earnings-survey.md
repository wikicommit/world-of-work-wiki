The Structure of Earnings Survey (SES) is a four-yearly European Union survey of earnings carried out under Regulation (EC) No 530/1999. Eurostat uses it as the methodological basis of the unadjusted [[DefinedTerm/gender-pay-gap]].

## Details

From reference year 2006 onwards, the unadjusted gender pay gap follows the SES methodology. The most recent SES reference years available are 2018 and 2022, and Eurostat computed the gender pay gap for those years on this basis. For the years in between, EU Member States provide Eurostat with gender pay gap estimates benchmarked on the SES results. Eurostat's 2024 gender pay gap data are provisional until benchmark figures taken from the SES become available in December 2026. The 2022 edition of the survey also underpins Eurostat's methodological study decomposing the unadjusted gender pay gap into differences in the characteristics of male and female employees and differences in the returns to those characteristics.
