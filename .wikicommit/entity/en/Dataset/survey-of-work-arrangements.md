The Survey of Work Arrangements is a survey conducted in 1991 and 1995. Both rounds were supplements to the Labour Force Survey for the month of November, with response rates of 94% and 90% respectively.

## Details

Statistics Canada's [[ScholarlyArticle/shift-work-and-health]] used the two rounds to trace trends in [[DefinedTerm/shift-work]] over the 1990s, estimating prevalence among full-year workers aged 18 to 54 from samples of 27,377 workers in 1991 and 21,250 in 1995, with standard errors based on simple-random-sampling formulas adjusted by a conservative design effect. Set beside the [[Dataset/national-population-health-survey]] and the [[Dataset/canadian-community-health-survey]], the survey showed the share of employed people with non-standard hours staying relatively stable at about 30% between 1991 and 2000/01: 28% of men and 29% of women in November 1991 and 33% and 30% in November 1995, among workers aged 18 to 54.
