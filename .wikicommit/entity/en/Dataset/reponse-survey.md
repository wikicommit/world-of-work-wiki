The Reponse survey (*Relations professionnelles et négociations d’entreprise*, workplace relations and company-level bargaining) is a survey by Dares, the research and statistics directorate of the French Ministry of Labour, that makes it possible to understand the dynamics of workplace relations and social dialogue between company management, staff representatives and employees.

## Details

The survey questions samples of management representatives and of employees from the same establishments. In the 2017 edition, carried out in the first half of the year, the samples were representative of 10.2 million employees who had worked for at least 15 months in nearly 200,000 private-sector (non-agricultural) establishments with more than 10 employees.

Because it has both an establishment part and an employee part, it can link the arrangements an establishment reports — for example, whether telework is governed by a collective agreement at establishment, company or group level, by a sector-level agreement, or only individually — to the working conditions and workplace relations its employees report. The Insee dossier [[Report/can-telework-improve-managers-working-conditions]] used it alongside the [[Dataset/sumer-survey]] in this way.
