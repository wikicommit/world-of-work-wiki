Registradas is an Argentine programme of economic support for hiring workers in private households (domestic workers), promoted jointly by the Ministry of Women, Genders and Diversity, the Ministry of Labour, Employment and Social Security, and the Ministry of Economy. It responds to the economic crisis in the sector by encouraging the recovery of jobs, extending labour rights, strengthening financial inclusion and correcting distributional distortions. One of its aims is to increase the formality of a sector with the highest levels of [[DefinedTerm/informal-employment]] in the economy: before the pandemic only 25.5% of its workers had registered employment.

The [[Organization/national-directorate-of-economy-equality-and-gender]] presents the programme as a way to narrow the gender income gap, because the sector is almost entirely female and its very low wages weigh heavily on women's average income; it expects that formalisation through the programme will raise the workers' incomes.

## Details

- **Who can take part.** Employers whose gross monthly income was below $175,000 in each of the 12 calendar months before 28 September 2021. The worker must be hired for 12 or more hours a week. Registration is done through the AFIP (federal tax agency) website.
- **What it pays.** The state transfers to each registered worker the equivalent of 50% or 30% of the monthly net wage the employer declared at registration, up to a maximum of $15,000. The money is paid into a salary account that Banco Nación opens in the worker's name.
- **Compatibility.** The benefit is compatible with the Universal Child Allowance, the Pregnancy Allowance, Potenciar Trabajo and Progresar, and incompatible with the non-contributory disability, mother-of-seven-children and old-age pensions.
- **Period.** Registration closes on 30 June 2022, and the state makes payments to the workers for six months.
