A variable working hours system (変形労働時間制) is a Japanese working-time arrangement under which, once it is set out in a labour–management agreement or in the work rules, an employer may have employees work beyond statutory working hours on specific days or in specific weeks, as long as working hours averaged over a set period do not exceed the statutory hours per week. It is an exception to the rule that employers may not, in principle, have employees work more than 8 hours a day or 40 hours a week.

## Usage

The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare distinguishes three forms by the length of the averaging unit: one month, one year and one week.
