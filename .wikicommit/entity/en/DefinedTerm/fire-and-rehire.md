"Fire and rehire" is the name given to a way of changing employees' contracts of employment without their agreement: where employees do not accept some or all of the proposed changes, the employer dismisses them and then rehires them, or offers to engage other employees, in substantively the same role so that the changes take effect.

## Usage

The term is used in UK employment law and policy. The [[Legislation/employment-rights-act-2025]] restricts the practice by amending the law on unfair dismissal: where employees are dismissed for failing to agree to certain changes to their contract of employment, the dismissal is treated as automatically unfair unless the employer can show evidence of financial difficulties and demonstrate that the need to change the contractual terms was unavoidable.
