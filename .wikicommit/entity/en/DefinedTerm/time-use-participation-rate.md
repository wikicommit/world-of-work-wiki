In Mexico's [[Dataset/mexico-national-time-use-survey]], the participation rate (*tasa de participación*) is the proportion of a given group that is actively involved in an activity or set of activities, relative to the whole group that could take part, expressed as a percentage. The ENUT distinguishes the social participation rate, calculated over the total population under study, from the participation rate in the activity, calculated over the population that takes part in that particular set of activities. It is paired with the average time (*tiempo promedio*), which divides the total weekly hours devoted to an activity by the number of people who did it — so average time describes those who take part, and the participation rate says how many they are.

## Usage

In [[Report/enut-2024-results-report]], for example, 98.3% of people aged 12 and over took part in unpaid domestic, care, community or volunteer work in 2024 and 60.2% in paid work for the market; participation in market work was 26.7 percentage points higher for men than for women, while women spent more hours on average than men on unpaid domestic, care, community and volunteer work.
