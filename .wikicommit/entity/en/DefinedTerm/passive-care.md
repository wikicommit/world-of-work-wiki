Passive care (*cuidados pasivos*) is, in the [[Dataset/mexico-national-time-use-survey]], a simultaneous or secondary activity of keeping watch over or looking after another person while carrying out a different, main activity. The ENUT's headline figures for unpaid care work exclude it, and the survey's 2024 edition made the concept more precise.

## Usage

According to [[Report/enut-2024-results-report]], counting passive care alongside active care raised the hours of care that both women and men gave in 2024 to each of the four groups of household members it reports, and widened the gap between them: women spent 33.4 hours a week caring for children aged 0 to 5 and men 14.8, and for household members with a chronic illness or disability women spent 23.8 hours against men's 13.1.
