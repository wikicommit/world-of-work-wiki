A flextime system (フレックスタイム制), in the Japanese statutory sense, is an arrangement in which employees decide for themselves when to start and finish work. The employer first provides for the system in its work rules or similar, and then a labour–management agreement sets the total working hours for a settlement period of up to three months, within a range where average weekly hours over that period do not exceed statutory working hours; within that total, each worker chooses their own starting and finishing times.
