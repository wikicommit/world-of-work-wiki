Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) is the minimum amount an employer in the UK is required to pay to an employee who is sick, where the employee meets the qualifying conditions. It is paid for a Period of Incapacity for Work (PIW).

## Usage

Before the [[Legislation/employment-rights-act-2025]], an employee had to earn at least the Lower Earnings Limit (£123 a week at the time the Explanatory Notes were written) to be eligible; a PIW had to be a period of four or more consecutive days of sickness, counting non-working days such as weekends; and SSP was not payable for the first three qualifying days, meaning days on which the employee would normally work.

The Act removes the earnings requirement, removes the rule that SSP is not payable for the first three qualifying days, and allows a PIW — and therefore eligibility for SSP — to arise from incapacity for a single day or longer. It sets the weekly rate at £118.75 or 80% of the employee's normal weekly earnings, whichever is lower; the £118.75 figure reflects the uprating from £116.75 that took effect on 6 April 2025. Enforcement of SSP is one of the functions brought together in the [[Organization/fair-work-agency]].
