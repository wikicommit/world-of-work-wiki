Total work time (*tiempo total de trabajo*, TTT) is the sum of the time a person devotes to all productive activities — both paid work for the market and [[DefinedTerm/unpaid-work]] — as defined in the [[Dataset/mexico-national-time-use-survey]]. Productive activities are those that produce goods or services for sale, for the household's own consumption, for running the household or for the community; their defining feature is that they could be delegated to someone else with the same result. They are set against personal (non-productive) activities, such as study, personal care and leisure, which benefit only the person doing them and cannot be delegated. In the ENUT the paid part includes time spent looking for work and travelling to work.

## Usage

According to [[Report/enut-2024-results-report]], total work time in Mexico averaged 59.6 hours a week in 2024 — 61.1 hours for women and 58.0 for men — even though men did more paid work: women spent 66.8% of their total work time on unpaid work and men 33.2%. The measure is also reported by state, locality size and population group, and as a gender gap in hours. Comparing it across survey editions requires a consistent definition: because emotional care was first measured in 2024, INEGI excludes it when comparing 2024 with 2014 and 2019, and on that basis total work time fell from 60.2 hours a week in 2019 to 55.8 in 2024.
