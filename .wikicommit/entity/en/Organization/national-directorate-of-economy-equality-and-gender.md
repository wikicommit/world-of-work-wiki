The National Directorate of Economy, Equality and Gender (Dirección Nacional de Economía, Igualdad y Género, DNEIyG) has worked since 2020 within Argentina's Ministry of Economy to incorporate a gender perspective into economic planning and policy. With the aim of reducing inequality gaps — in particular gender gaps in the economic sphere — it produces indicators and diagnoses, coordinates and carries out specific actions, and promotes public policies with a gender perspective.

## Activities & Products

With its report on the fourth quarter of 2021, [[Report/gender-gaps-in-the-argentine-economy-q4-2021]], the directorate began a quarterly series tracking economic indicators from a gender perspective: each issue presents basic indicators of gaps in time and care, in the labour market and in income, together with a section on a specific population or topic.
