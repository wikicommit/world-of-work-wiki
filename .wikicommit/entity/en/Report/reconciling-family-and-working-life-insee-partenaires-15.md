*Conciliation vie familiale et vie professionnelle* ("Reconciling family life and working life") is issue No 15 of the *Insee partenaires* collection, published on 12 July 2011 by Insee together with the DJSCS and the DRDFEH. It looks at how people combine paid work with family life, in both directions: how work weighs on domestic life, and how family life weighs on work.

According to the study, after a working day nearly half of people say they are frequently in difficulty when it comes to carrying out domestic tasks. Mothers raising their child or children alone are the most affected, and couples in which both partners are employed and who have children also frequently report difficulties. In the other direction, few working people consider that their family life affects their ability to "work well". The study adds that employers nevertheless often show "flexibility" in dealing with unforeseen events.
