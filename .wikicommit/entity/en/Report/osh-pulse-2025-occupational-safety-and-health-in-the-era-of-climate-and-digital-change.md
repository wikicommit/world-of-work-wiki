This 65-page EU-OSHA report, published on 23 September 2025, presents the findings of the "OSH Pulse 2025" survey, which EU-OSHA commissioned to gain insights into the state of occupational safety and health (OSH) in the era of climate and digital change. A representative sample of more than 28,000 employed workers was interviewed in March and April 2025 in each EU Member State and in Iceland, Norway and Switzerland.

The survey explored workers' experiences and perceptions in four areas: the use of digital technologies for work, psychosocial factors ([[DefinedTerm/psychosocial-risks]]), climate change-related risks, and health outcomes related to work. EU-OSHA published the report alongside a 16-page summary, three infographics on climate change, digitalisation and mental health at work, and two-page national reports for each country covered.
