Wie gaan meer of minder werken? ("Who are going to work more or fewer hours?") is an article by two authors published on 3 October 2025 in the Statistische Trends series of the Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek (CBS). It asks which groups of employees go on to work more hours and which fewer: whether this differs between employees with a permanent and a flexible contract, between men and women, between age groups, and between people with large and small jobs. To answer this it looks at 6.9 million employees who had a job in the fourth quarter of 2021 and at how far their working hours had changed two years later.

## Details

The article's main findings are that most employees (53 per cent) keep working the same number of hours. Employees who do change their working hours relatively often end up in a larger part-time job of 28 to 32 hours ([[DefinedTerm/part-time-employment]]), and this differs by sector. Employees under 25 are the most flexible in the hours they work. Women in all age groups both increase and reduce their hours more often than men in the same age group.
