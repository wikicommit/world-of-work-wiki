Onderbenutte deeltijders: van meer willen werken naar meer gaan werken ("Underemployed part-time workers: from wanting to work more to working more") is an article by two authors published on 10 October 2024 in the Statistische Trends series of the Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek (CBS). Using new figures from the Labour Force Survey (Enquête beroepsbevolking, EBB), it asks how many people want and are able to work more but do not, what characterises these underemployed part-time workers, how many of them actually go on to work more hours, and how much this unused labour potential contributes to the growth of total working hours.

## Details

The main findings are that in 2023, 48% of underemployed part-time workers were in education, and more than half (54%) of those in education had an on-call contract. Underemployed part-time workers often work in occupations where part-time work is usual, such as service occupations and transport and logistics. On average 29% of them were working more hours a quarter later in 2023, considerably more than the rest of the employed labour force (7%). Their contribution to the growth of total working hours in 2023 was nevertheless limited: per quarter, unemployed people contributed more than four times as many full-time equivalents as underemployed part-time workers.
