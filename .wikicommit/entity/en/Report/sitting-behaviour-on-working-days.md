Zitgedrag op werkdagen ("Sitting behaviour on working days") is an article by two authors published on 20 August 2025 in the Statistische Trends series of the Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek (CBS). It starts from the observation that employees sit for a large part of their working day, and that because sitting a lot and moving little go together with health risks — and can therefore bring societal costs — it is important to have a good picture of how much employees sit. Since the number of hours spent sitting in free time also matters, the article gives an overview of employees' sitting during an average working day, both at work and in their free time.

## Details

In 2024 employees sat on average 4.5 hours per working day while working, 1.1 hours while commuting and 3.3 hours in their free time. Of these, only free-time sitting on working days has risen in recent years, slightly, and mainly between 2017 and 2022.

The number of hours spent sitting at work differs between occupations and is associated with working from home, screen work and working part-time or full-time. The number of hours spent sitting in free time on a working day is associated with employees' age, with employees aged 35 to 45 spending the fewest hours sitting. The article finds no pronounced link between sitting at work and sitting in free time on a working day.
