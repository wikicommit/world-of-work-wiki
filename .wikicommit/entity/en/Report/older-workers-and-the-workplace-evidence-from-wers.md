*Older workers and the workplace: evidence from the Workplace Employment Relations Survey* is a research report published by the UK Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) on 2 February 2017 as research report RR939 (ISBN 978 1 911003 63 2), with a separate three-page summary. It was written by Lucy Stokes, Alex Bryson, Helen Bewley and John Forth of the National Institute of Economic and Social Research. Drawing on the Workplace Employment Relations Survey, it examines the working experiences of older individuals and the effects of changes in age composition on workplace performance.

The research is quantitative and has three aims: to better understand the labour market participation of older workers in the UK, including the locations and sectors they work in; to assess the effects of workplace policies and practices on older workers and their experiences; and to evaluate the effects of employing older workers on workplace performance.

## Details

DWP describes the report as contributing towards filling known evidence gaps, such as the importance of equal opportunities policies for the outcomes of older workers, and as the first time that an analysis of the effects of older workers on workplace performance has been conducted using UK data. It is one of four DWP research reports contributing to the department's Fuller Working Lives evidence base, and DWP says it will help the department identify, promote and implement evidence-backed policies to help employers recruit and retain older workers.
