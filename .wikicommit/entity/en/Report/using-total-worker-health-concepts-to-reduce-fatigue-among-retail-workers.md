*Workplace Solutions: Using Total Worker Health® Concepts to Reduce Fatigue among Retail Workers* is an eight-page document published by the National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH) in 2018 as DHHS (NIOSH) Publication No. 2019-102, in its Workplace Solutions series. NIOSH's description of the document notes that retail workers in the United States have in recent years engaged in more [[DefinedTerm/shift-work]] and longer work hours, and that these schedules can result in [[DefinedTerm/work-related-fatigue]] and related health issues.

Through its [[DefinedTerm/total-worker-health]] (TWH) Program, NIOSH recommends an integrated approach to fatigue in retail work — one that protects workers from work-related injury and illness and helps them advance their overall health and well-being, on and off the job. The approach addresses factors affecting both the workplace and home life, including autonomy at work, workload and environmental factors, and the document describes organisational practices related to scheduling, flexibility and the work environment that can help address the causes and consequences of fatigue among retail workers.
