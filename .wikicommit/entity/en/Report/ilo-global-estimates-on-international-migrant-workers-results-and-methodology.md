*ILO Global Estimates on International Migrant Workers – Results and Methodology* is a report published by the International Labour Organization on 30 June 2021. It maps the current state of labour migration and the key characteristics of migrant workers in the world. This third edition presents the most recent estimates of the stock of international migrant workers, disaggregated by age, sex, country-income group and region, and sets out the methodology used to produce them.

The reference year of the estimates is 2019, so the report predates the onset of the COVID-19 crisis, which has affected the magnitude and characteristics of international labour migration. The ILO presents the estimates as a benchmark against which COVID-19-driven changes can be analysed in the future.

## Details

The report is published periodically. The ILO describes it as providing information on recent trends in labour migration, and so as contributing to the Sustainable Development Goals and supporting policymaking at country, regional and global levels. It is available as a PDF, with ISBN 9789220346563 for the print edition and 9789220346570 for the web PDF; the ILO also issued an executive summary and an infographic of the estimates.
