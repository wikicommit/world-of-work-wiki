*How to tackle work-related stress* is an HSE leaflet for employers, first published in October 2009 as INDG430. It explains the [[DefinedTerm/management-standards-for-work-related-stress]] and how to use them to identify and address causes of [[DefinedTerm/work-related-stress]] in an organization.

The leaflet distinguishes motivating pressure from stress that can result when demands become too great and people feel unable to cope. It presents the Management Standards as an organizational approach involving managers, employees and representatives, rather than a focus solely on individual employees.

## Details

The six standards cover demands, control, support, relationships, role and change. For each, the leaflet describes desired workplace conditions, such as achievable demands within agreed hours, a say in how work is done, access to support and clear communication during change.

Its five-step [[HowTo/apply-the-hse-management-standards-for-work-related-stress]] process moves from identifying risks and gathering several forms of workplace data to discussing solutions, implementing action plans and reviewing their effects. The leaflet also emphasizes preparation, senior management commitment and line managers' role in recognizing problems and supporting staff.
