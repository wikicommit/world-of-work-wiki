Práci z domova umožňuje 59 podniků ze 100 ("59 enterprises out of 100 allow working from home") is an article published on 20 May 2025 in Statistika&My, the information magazine of the Czech Statistical Office (ČSÚ). It draws on 2024 data from ČSÚ's annual statistical survey on the use of ICT in the business sector, carried out on a sample of enterprises with 10 or more employees in selected industries; for international comparability, agriculture, mining, finance and insurance, public administration, education, health and social care, and cultural activities are excluded. Its headline finding is that a fifth of employees used home office in Czechia in 2024 and that online work meetings took place in about half of enterprises. The article argues that, after the boom brought by the COVID-19 pandemic, the option of [[DefinedTerm/working-from-home]] is unlikely to disappear for jobs whose nature allows it.

## Details

**Enterprises allowing work from home.** In 2024, 59 % of enterprises allowed employees to work from home at least sometimes: 92 % of large enterprises (more than 250 employees), 79 % of medium-sized ones (50-250) and 53 % of small ones. The share was highest in IT services (97 %), research and development and management consultancy (94 % each) and programming and broadcasting (91 %), and lowest in food and beverage services (about a fifth), with retail (45 %), construction (44 %) and other administrative and support activities (46 %) also below average. In manufacturing, part of administrative work had been moved to home office, especially in the chemical and pharmaceutical and metallurgical industries. The share was 30 % in 2014, doubled to 60 % in 2020 during the pandemic, fell slightly to 54 % in 2021 after restrictions were relaxed, and stood at 59 % in 2024, almost the pandemic level.

**Employees working from home.** The share of employees in enterprises with 10 or more employees who worked from home rose from 4 % in 2014 to 20 % during the pandemic, fell to 15 % a year later and reached 21 % in 2024, with little difference by enterprise size. It was highest in information and communication — over 85 % in IT — and over three quarters in legal and accounting activities (79 %), management consultancy (77 %) and telecommunications (76 %), and lowest in food and beverage services (3 %) and accommodation (10 %). In construction and manufacturing an average of 14 % could work from home at least sometimes.

**Online meetings and remote access.** In 2024, 47 % of Czech enterprises held online work meetings — 39 % of small, 74 % of medium-sized and 94 % of large enterprises — below the EU-27 average of 53 %; Finland had the highest share (85 %). Remote access to work e-mail was offered by 72 % of enterprises (59 % in 2014), the fourth-lowest value in the EU against an EU-27 average of 81 %, and remote access to documents, applications or information systems by 63 % (46 % in 2016), against an EU-27 average of 74 %.
