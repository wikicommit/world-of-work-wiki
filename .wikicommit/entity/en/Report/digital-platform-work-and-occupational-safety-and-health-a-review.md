This 43-page report, published by EU-OSHA on 13 October 2021, reviews digital platform work from the point of view of occupational safety and health (OSH). Starting from the observation that increasing numbers of workers are employed through [[DefinedTerm/digital-labour-platform]]s, it defines and characterises digital platform work and [[DefinedTerm/digital-platform-worker]]s, and looks at the OSH risks associated with this kind of work — arising, for instance, from digital surveillance, performance pressure and [[DefinedTerm/job-insecurity]].

The report highlights that most platform workers have "self-employed" status, which means they are often not protected by EU or Member State OSH regulation, with implications for how OSH risks are prevented and managed. It also discusses new initiatives and legislation that aim to improve working conditions and protect platform workers. EU-OSHA published it together with a shorter policy brief on the same subject.
