De werk-privébalans van vaders met jonge kinderen ("The work–life balance of fathers with young children") is an article by a single author in the Statistische Trends series of the Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek (CBS), published on 27 March 2019. Starting from the point that having children means parents have to make choices about combining paid work and care, it centres on fathers of children up to twelve years old.

The article asks how fathers' working patterns compare with those of men without children, and whether the number of children in the family or the age of the youngest child makes a difference. It also asks how often fathers make use of options such as part-time work ([[DefinedTerm/part-time-employment]]) or working from home ([[DefinedTerm/working-from-home]]), what their partner's working week looks like, how many fathers use leave schemes to care for their children, and whether they find that work and family responsibilities sometimes clash ([[DefinedTerm/work-life-balance]]).

## Details

The article is organised in chapters on fathers' labour market position, their working hours, the use of leave schemes by fathers of babies, parental leave, working from home, and fathers' own views on (their autonomy in) the work–life balance, followed by a summary.
