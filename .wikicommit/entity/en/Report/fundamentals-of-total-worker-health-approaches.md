*Fundamentals of Total Worker Health Approaches* is a NIOSH workbook published in December 2016 as DHHS (NIOSH) Publication No. 2017-112. It presents [[DefinedTerm/total-worker-health]] as an approach that connects protection from work hazards with efforts to improve worker well-being.

The workbook is intended as a practical starting point for employers, workers, labor representatives, and others developing workplace safety and health programs. Its five [[DefinedTerm/defining-elements-of-total-worker-health]] are accompanied by examples, questions, a self-assessment worksheet, and an action-plan worksheet. NIOSH describes the workbook as a way to assess current efforts, identify changes, and revisit progress, rather than as a uniform program for every workplace.

## Details

The five elements cover leadership commitment, work design and hazard reduction, worker engagement, confidentiality and privacy, and coordination of organizational systems. The work-design discussion includes the [[DefinedTerm/total-worker-health-hierarchy-of-controls]], which prioritizes changes to working conditions before individual education or behavior change. The worksheets support the [[HowTo/assess-and-plan-total-worker-health-improvements]] process described in the user guide.
