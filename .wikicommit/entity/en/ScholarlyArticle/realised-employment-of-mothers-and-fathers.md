"Realisierte Erwerbstätigkeit von Müttern und Vätern zur Vereinbarkeit von Familie und Beruf" ("Realised employment of mothers and fathers in reconciling family and work") is an article by Matthias Keller and Irene Kahle published in issue 3/2018 of *WISTA – Wirtschaft und Statistik*, the journal of Germany's Federal Statistical Office (Destatis), dated 13 June 2018. It starts from the observation that organising family life usually requires parents to compromise between caring for children and earning a living, especially for parents of children under three, who need a particularly large amount of care ([[DefinedTerm/work-life-balance]]).

The article presents figures on parents' employment from the 2016 Microcensus, focusing on mothers and fathers with at least one minor child. It uses the concept of **realised employment** (*realisierte Erwerbstätigkeit*), under which people who have interrupted their employment because of maternity protection or parental leave are not counted as employed.

## Key Points

- Mothers, above all mothers of small children, are less often in realised employment than fathers.
- Employed mothers work part time considerably more often than fathers ([[DefinedTerm/part-time-employment]]).
