The Act Promoting Measures to Prevent Death and Injury from Overwork (Act No. 100 of 2014) is the Japanese law that frames the government's policy on [[DefinedTerm/karoshi]]. It gives "death and injury from overwork" a statutory definition and obliges the government to report annually to the Diet on the situation and on what it has done to prevent it.

## Details

**Definition.** Article 2 defines death and injury from overwork as death due to cerebrovascular disease or heart disease brought on by an overload of work; death by suicide due to a mental disorder brought on by an intense psychological burden at work; or cerebrovascular disease, heart disease or a mental disorder brought on by such work-related causes.

**Annual reports.** Article 6 requires the government to submit to the Diet every year a report summarising death and injury from overwork in Japan and describing the implementation of the measures it has taken to prevent it. This report is the statutory White Paper on Measures to Prevent Karoshi, etc., whose 2025 edition was the tenth ([[Report/white-paper-on-measures-to-prevent-karoshi-2025]]). The white paper also refers to the Outline of Measures to Prevent Death and Injury from Overwork (Cabinet decision of 2 August 2024), which designates industries and sectors as priority targets for survey and research.
