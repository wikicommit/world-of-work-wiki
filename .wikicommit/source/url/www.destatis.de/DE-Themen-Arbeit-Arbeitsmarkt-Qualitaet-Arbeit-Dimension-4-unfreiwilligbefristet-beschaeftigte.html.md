## Summary

Destatis's quality-of-employment indicator on involuntary temporary employment: the share of employees aged 25 and over who are on a fixed-term contract because they could not find a permanent job. In 2025 this was 8.2% (7.7% of women, 8.8% of men, and 16.6% among unskilled assistant workers), based on the Labour Force Survey integrated in the Mikrozensus; the page notes that answer categories added in 2021 make results from 2021 onwards not comparable with earlier years.
