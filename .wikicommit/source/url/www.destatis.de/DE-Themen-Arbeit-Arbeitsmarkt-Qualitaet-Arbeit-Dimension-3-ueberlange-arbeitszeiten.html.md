## Summary

This Destatis page in Germany's indicators on the quality of work reports the share of full-time workers who usually work 49 hours or more a week, a threshold it says follows international conventions. In 2025 the share was 7.1% (8.4% of men, 4.5% of women); it rises with age and is much higher among managers, skilled agricultural workers and above all the self-employed (35.6%, and 44.5% of those with employees) than among employees (4.1%). The figures come from the Labour Force Survey integrated into the Microcensus, whose 2020 redesign limits comparability with earlier years.
