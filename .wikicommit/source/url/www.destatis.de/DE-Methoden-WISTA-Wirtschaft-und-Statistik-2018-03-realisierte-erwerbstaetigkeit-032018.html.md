## Summary

This Destatis publication page presents an article from the June 2018 issue of WISTA – Wirtschaft und Statistik on the employment of mothers and fathers in Germany as it bears on reconciling family and work. Using 2016 Microcensus data and the concept of "realised employment", which does not count people whose employment is interrupted by maternity protection or parental leave as employed, it examines parents with at least one minor child. It finds that mothers, above all those with small children, are less often in realised employment than fathers and are considerably more often working part time.
