## Summary

This chapter of Statistics Netherlands' (CBS) Emancipation Monitor 2024 describes how couples with children in the Netherlands combine paid work with care for children, the household and sick relatives. Most parents would prefer an equal division of work and childcare, yet in practice the "one-and-a-half earner" pattern of a full-time father and a part-time mother dominates, and women's income from work is 35% lower six years after the birth of a first child while men's is not reduced. The chapter also covers attitudes to working hours and childcare, use of formal childcare, parental and birth leave, and how employees combine work with informal care for sick relatives.
