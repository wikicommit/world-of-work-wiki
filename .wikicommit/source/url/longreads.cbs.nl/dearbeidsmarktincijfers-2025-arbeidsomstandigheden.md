## Summary

Chapter 4 ("Arbeidsomstandigheden") of Statistics Netherlands' publication "De arbeidsmarkt in cijfers 2025" describes the working conditions of employees and self-employed people in the Netherlands, mainly from the 2025 Netherlands Working Conditions Survey (NEA) and Self-employed Labour Survey (ZEA) run with TNO, and CBS's quarterly sickness absence survey. It reports physical strain (37% of employees regularly doing physically heavy work), high work pressure (31% of employees, down from 38% in 2015), emotionally demanding work, autonomy, discrimination and undesirable behaviour, rising work-related mental fatigue (21% of employees), a sickness absence rate of 5.4% in 2025, work accidents and occupational diseases, with breakdowns by sector, sex and age.
