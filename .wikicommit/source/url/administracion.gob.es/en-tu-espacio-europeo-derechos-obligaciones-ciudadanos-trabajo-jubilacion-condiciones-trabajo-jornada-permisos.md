## Summary

A Spanish Government information page on the Your European Space portal summarising the rules on working hours, overtime, paid leave, annual holidays, temporary incapacity and medical examinations for employees in Spain, based mainly on Articles 34–38 of the Workers' Statute and the Labour Guide. It sets out, among other things, a maximum of 40 hours of effective work as a weekly average over the year, at least 12 hours' rest between working days, a 9-hour ordinary daily maximum, a mandatory daily record of working time, an annual cap of 80 overtime hours and at least 30 calendar days of paid annual holiday.
