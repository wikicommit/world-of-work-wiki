## Summary

An Office for National Statistics article, released on 22 January 2024, analysing why average weekly hours worked in the UK fell from 33.1 in 1998 to 31.8 in 2022, and by 0.3 hours since 2019. Using shift-share analysis of Labour Force Survey data and regressions on Annual Population Survey microdata, it attributes the fall mainly to shorter hours among men working full-time and to the growing shares of women and older workers in employment. It also finds that changes in average hours had a large effect on GDP growth during the COVID-19 pandemic but only a small effect on long-term growth.
