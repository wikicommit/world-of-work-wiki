## Summary

An Office for National Statistics article published on 11 November 2024 that examines which workers in Great Britain follow a hybrid working pattern and how working from home affects time use. Drawing on the Opinions and Lifestyle Survey, the Time Use Survey and the Business Insights and Conditions Survey, it reports that 28% of working adults were hybrid working in autumn 2024, that hybrid working was more common among workers aged 30 and over, parents, managers and professionals and those with degree-level qualifications, and that it was most common in the information and communication industry and least common in accommodation and food services.
