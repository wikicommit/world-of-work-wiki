## Summary

An experimental Office for National Statistics analysis, released on 4 December 2019, of job quality among UK employees in 2018 using reweighted earnings data from the Annual Population Survey. It examines three indicators — working "satisfactory hours" (48 hours or fewer and not underemployed), having a "desired contract", and not being in low pay (below two-thirds of UK or local median hourly pay) — by country, city region, sex, age, ethnicity, qualification, occupation and industry. It also combines them into a composite measure of "quality work", while cautioning that the composite is not a definitive measure of job quality.
