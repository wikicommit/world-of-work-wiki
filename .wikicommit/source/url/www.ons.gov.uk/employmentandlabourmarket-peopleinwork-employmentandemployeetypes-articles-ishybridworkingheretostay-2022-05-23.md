## Summary

An Office for National Statistics article, published on 23 May 2022, on whether hybrid working will persist in Great Britain after COVID-19 work-from-home guidance was lifted. Using the Opinions and Lifestyle Survey and the Business Insights and Conditions Survey, it reports that 84% of workers who had worked from home during the pandemic planned to hybrid work, that hybrid working rose from 13% to 24% of workers between February and May 2022, and that it was most common among higher earners and workers aged 30 to 49. It also covers the benefits and drawbacks homeworkers reported and the share of businesses planning to use homeworking permanently, by industry.
