## Summary

An Office for National Statistics article released on 7 February 2018 that analyses the growth of self-employment in the UK, which rose from 3.3 million people (12.0% of the labour force) in 2001 to 4.8 million (15.1%) in 2017, driven by those working alone or with a partner but no employees. Using the Labour Force Survey, the Family Resources Survey and the Wealth and Assets Survey, it compares the characteristics, income and wealth of the self-employed with employees, finding lower and slower-growing typical earnings among the full-time self-employed, more property wealth but much less private pension wealth.
