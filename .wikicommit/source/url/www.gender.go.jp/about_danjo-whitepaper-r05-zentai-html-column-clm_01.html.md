## Summary

Column 1 of the Reiwa 5 edition of Japan's White Paper on Gender Equality, published by the Cabinet Office's Gender Equality Bureau, uses OECD time-use data to compare how women and men in 11 countries across North America, Europe, the Nordic countries and Asia spend their time. It finds that Japan has the second-longest combined paid and unpaid working time after the United States, the longest paid working time, the most lopsided division of paid and unpaid work between women and men, and the shortest sleep of the 11 countries. It argues that reducing Japanese men's long paid working hours is a precondition for men taking on more unpaid work and for advancing a gender-equal society.
