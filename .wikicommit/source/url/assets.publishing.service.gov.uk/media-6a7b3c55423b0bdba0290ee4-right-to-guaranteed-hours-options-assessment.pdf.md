## Summary

This Options Assessment by the UK Department for Business and Trade accompanies a consultation on the secondary legislation that will implement the right to guaranteed hours created by the Employment Rights Act 2025, under which employers must offer qualifying workers on zero-hours or low-hours contracts, and end hirers qualifying agency workers, a contract guaranteeing the hours they worked over a reference period. It sets out the case against "one-sided flexibility", the options being consulted on for the hours threshold (government preference 8 to 20 hours a week), regularity requirements, the calculation of offers and the length of reference periods (preference 12 weeks), and estimates the monetised costs to business — an equivalent annual net direct cost of £100 million to £450 million — while treating the benefits to workers as significant but unquantifiable.
