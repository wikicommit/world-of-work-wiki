## Summary

This 2011 issue of the Robert Koch Institute's GBE kompakt series presents results from the German Health Update (GEDA) 2010 telephone survey on how often employed women and men aged 18 to 64 in Germany face physical, environmental and psychosocial strains at work and how far they perceive their own work as endangering their health. About 14% of women and 20% of men saw their health as strongly or very strongly endangered by their work, with full-time workers, manual workers and certain sectors reporting this more often, and a poor working climate and time or performance pressure most closely associated with that perception.
