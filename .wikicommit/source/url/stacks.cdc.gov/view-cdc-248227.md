## Summary

A CDC Stacks catalogue record for a 2013 Sleep Research Society Bulletin article by NIOSH researchers on NIOSH's efforts to prevent workplace hazards linked to inadequate sleep. Its description reports that about 15% of US full-time workers have shift schedules outside daytime hours, that 19% of Americans worked 48 hours or more a week in 2010, and that short sleep duration among full-time workers rose from 24% (1985–1990) to almost 30% (2004–2007), with higher rates among night workers, and it describes shift work and long working hours as recognised occupational hazards linked to errors, accidents and disease.
