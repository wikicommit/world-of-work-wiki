## Summary

A CDC Stacks catalogue record for a 2018 NIOSH Workplace Solutions document (DHHS (NIOSH) Publication No. 2019-102) on using Total Worker Health concepts to reduce fatigue among retail workers. Its description notes that US retail workers have engaged in more shift work and longer hours, which can cause fatigue and related health problems, and that NIOSH recommends an integrated approach addressing factors at work and at home — autonomy, workload and environmental factors — through organisational practices on scheduling, flexibility and the work environment.
