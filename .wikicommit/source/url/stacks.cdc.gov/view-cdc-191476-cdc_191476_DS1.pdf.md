## Summary

A 2006 report in the American Journal of Industrial Medicine by the NIOSH-convened National Occupational Research Agenda (NORA) Long Work Hours Team that proposes a research agenda and conceptual framework for studying long working hours, covering their determinants, moderating factors, and outcomes for workers, families, employers and communities. It argues that studies need fuller descriptions of work schedules, workers and work environments, a wider range of outcomes, more attention to vulnerable groups and public-safety occupations, and more development and evaluation of interventions.
