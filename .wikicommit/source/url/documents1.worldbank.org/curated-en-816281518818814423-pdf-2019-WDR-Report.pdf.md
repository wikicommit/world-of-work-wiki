## Summary

The World Bank's World Development Report 2019 examines how technology is changing work, firms and the skills that labour markets reward, concluding that fears of large-scale technology-driven job losses are on balance unfounded, while persistent informality remains the greatest labour-market challenge in emerging economies. It argues for investment in human capital from early childhood through tertiary and adult learning, a guaranteed social minimum and social insurance less dependent on formal wage employment, labour regulation rebalanced as protection improves, and new revenue to finance a renewed social contract.
