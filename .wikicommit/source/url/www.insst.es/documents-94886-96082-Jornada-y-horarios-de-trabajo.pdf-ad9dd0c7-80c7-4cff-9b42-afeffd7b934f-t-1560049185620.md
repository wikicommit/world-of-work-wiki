## Summary

This report by Spain's National Institute for Safety and Hygiene at Work (INSHT; prepared October 2013, published August 2014) analyses working hours and schedules using the VII National Survey of Working Conditions (2011, 8,892 workers), comparing results with the 2007 survey. It covers the length of the working week, part-time work, the habitual extension of working hours with and without compensation, usual schedules, shift and night work and their associations with health complaints and accidents, weekend and holiday work, and how well schedules fit family and social commitments, and closes with a non-exhaustive list of Spanish, European and ILO provisions on working time.
