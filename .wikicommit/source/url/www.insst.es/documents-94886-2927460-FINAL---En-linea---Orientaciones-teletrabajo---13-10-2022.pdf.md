## Summary

A technical document published by Spain's National Institute for Safety and Health at Work (INSST) in July 2022 giving guidance on managing ergonomic and psychosocial risks in telework under Spain's Remote Work Act (Law 10/2021). It sets out the legal framework and a preventive management approach built on job design and primary prevention, suitability assessment, training, risk assessment, two-way communication and periodic review; describes ergonomic risk factors (postures, repetitive movements, sedentary behaviour, visual fatigue, mental workload) and psychosocial risk factors (working time, autonomy, workload, role, social support, work–life balance) together with their consequences, such as technostress and cyberbullying, and preventive measures; and includes checklists and questionnaires as annexes.
