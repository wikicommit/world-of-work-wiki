## Summary

Managing psychosocial risks at work (April 2025) is WorkSafe New Zealand's guidance for all businesses on meeting their duty under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, which defines health as both physical and mental health, to manage psychosocial risks. It explains what psychosocial hazards and risks are, grouping common hazards under work design, social factors and the work environment, and sets out how to identify hazards and assess risks, choose control measures that prioritise eliminating risk, work with other businesses, monitor and review controls, and respond when workers are harmed. Appendices provide a glossary, examples of risk and protective factors, and summaries of the Act's duties, the "so far as is reasonably practicable" test, overlapping duties and worker engagement.
