## Summary

WorkSafe New Zealand's Fatigue quick guide (July 2017) describes fatigue as a state of physical and/or mental exhaustion that reduces alertness and can lead to errors, incidents and injuries, caused by work schedules (long or irregular hours, night and shift work), sleep disruption, environmental conditions, physical and mental work demands, and emotional wellbeing. It sets out how businesses must manage fatigue risks under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 — through work scheduling, roster design that allows sleep and recovery, managing workplace conditions and workloads, and a workplace fatigue policy — and what workers should do, including recognising the signs of fatigue and reporting it. It ends with practical steps for minimising fatigue and a checklist for identifying fatigue risks.
