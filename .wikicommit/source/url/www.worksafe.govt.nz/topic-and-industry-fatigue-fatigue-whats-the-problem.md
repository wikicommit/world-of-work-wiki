## Summary

WorkSafe New Zealand's web guidance page on fatigue defines it as a state of physical and/or mental exhaustion that reduces a person's ability to work safely and effectively, and lists its causes: work schedules (hours, night and shift work, breaks between shifts), sleep disruption, environmental conditions, physical and mental work demands, and emotional well-being. It sets out examples of how businesses can eliminate or minimise the risk — achievable demands in relation to agreed hours, matching skills to job demands, control over the pace of work, manager capability, counselling and consultation — and stresses involving and training workers.
