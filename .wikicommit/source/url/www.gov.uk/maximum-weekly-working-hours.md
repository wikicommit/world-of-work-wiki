## Summary

GOV.UK's overview of maximum weekly working hours states that workers cannot work more than 48 hours a week on average, normally averaged over 17 weeks, unless they opt out, and that workers under 18 cannot work more than 8 hours a day or 40 hours a week. It lists the kinds of jobs in which people may have to work more than the 48-hour average and notes that employers must keep some records of working hours.
