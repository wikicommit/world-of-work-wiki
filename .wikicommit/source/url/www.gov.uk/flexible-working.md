## Summary

GOV.UK's overview of flexible working explains that all employees have a legal right, from their first day in a job, to request a change to their working hours, start and finish times, working days or place of work, with different rules applying in Northern Ireland. Employers must deal with requests in a "reasonable manner", may refuse only for a good business reason, and can be taken to an employment tribunal if they do not handle a request reasonably.
