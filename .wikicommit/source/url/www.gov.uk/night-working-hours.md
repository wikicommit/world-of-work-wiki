## Summary

GOV.UK's guide to night working hours for employers explains who counts as a night worker (someone who regularly works at least 3 hours in the night period, normally 11pm to 6am) and the limits that apply: night workers must not work more than an average of 8 hours in 24, usually averaged over 17 weeks, with no opt-out, and those whose work involves special hazards or heavy mental or physical strain may not exceed 8 hours in any 24-hour period. It also covers National Minimum Wage rules for night and sleep-in shifts, restrictions on night work for 16- and 17-year-olds, and employers' duty to keep records of night workers' hours for at least 2 years.
