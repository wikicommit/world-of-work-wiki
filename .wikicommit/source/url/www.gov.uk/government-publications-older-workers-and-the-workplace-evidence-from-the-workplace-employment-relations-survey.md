## Summary

This is the Department for Work and Pensions' publication page for "Older workers and the workplace: evidence from the Workplace Employment Relations Survey" (research report RR939, published 2 February 2017), written by researchers at the National Institute of Economic and Social Research. The quantitative research uses the Workplace Employment Relations Survey to examine the labour market participation and working experiences of older workers in the UK, the effects of workplace policies and practices on them, and the effects of employing older workers on workplace performance. It is one of four DWP research reports contributing to the Fuller Working Lives evidence base for 2017.
