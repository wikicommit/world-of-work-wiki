## Summary

GOV.UK's guide to holiday entitlement explains that almost all workers are legally entitled to 5.6 weeks' paid holiday a year, equivalent to 28 days for someone working a five-day week and capped at 28 days. It covers how the entitlement applies to part-time, irregular-hours and part-year workers, the treatment of bank holidays and extra contractual leave, and workers' rights to build up leave while on sick, maternity, paternity or adoption leave.
