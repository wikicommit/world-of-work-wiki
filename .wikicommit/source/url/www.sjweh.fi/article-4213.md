## Summary

A 2025 editorial in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health arguing that remote work has become the new normal but that research on it has knowledge gaps. It discusses inconsistent terminology and self-reported measures, the dominance of pandemic-era cross-sectional studies, the need for regulation such as the right to disconnect, occupational safety and health challenges of working at home, and predicts that the debate will shift from remote work to employee-oriented flexibility.
