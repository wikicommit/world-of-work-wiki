## Summary

A 2018 systematic review and meta-analysis in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health that combined ten published prospective cohort studies with unpublished individual-participant data from 18 studies to quantify the relation between long working hours (mostly defined as 55 or more hours a week) and the onset of depressive symptoms. Across 189,729 participants from 35 countries it found an overall association of 1.14 (95% CI 1.03–1.25), with a moderate association in Asia, a weaker one in Europe and none in North America or Australia.
