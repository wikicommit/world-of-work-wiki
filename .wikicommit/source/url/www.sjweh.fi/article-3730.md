## Summary

A 2018 within-subject study in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health linking survey responses of 7727 hospital employees to their daily working-hour records. Increases in the share of night shifts, short shift intervals and more than two consecutive night shifts were associated with increased fatigue, difficulty falling asleep or longer sleep, most strongly among workers aged 50 or over, and the authors suggest fewer night shifts and quick returns and quickly forward-rotating shift systems may reduce fatigue.
