## Summary

An editorial in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health (2021) on why extended working hours matter as a risk factor for work-related injuries. It introduces a systematic review in the same issue that linked long daily (>12 hours) and weekly (>55 hours) working hours to more safety incidents, and discusses the methodological challenges of the field — entanglement with quick returns, night work and compressed work weeks, causality, regional differences — and the value of day-to-day payroll data.
