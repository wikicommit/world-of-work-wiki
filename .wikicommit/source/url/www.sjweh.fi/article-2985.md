## Summary

A 2010 article in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health reporting a questionnaire survey of 336 junior doctors on schedules compliant with the European Working Time Directive. Seven consecutive nights, only one rest day after nights, frequent on-calls and inter-shift intervals under 10 hours were associated with greater fatigue, work–life interference or psychological strain, identifying schedule-design parameters beyond those the Directive specifies.
