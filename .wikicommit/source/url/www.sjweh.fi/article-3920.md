## Summary

A 2020 discussion paper in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health in which 15 shift work researchers, after a workshop in early 2020, give consensus recommendations for scheduling night shifts to reduce health and safety risks. They recommend at most three consecutive night shifts, shift intervals of at least 11 hours and night shifts of at most 9 hours, and that pregnant women work no more than one night shift a week.
