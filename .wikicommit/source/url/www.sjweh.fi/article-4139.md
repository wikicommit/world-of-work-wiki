## Summary

A 2024 cohort study in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health using five-year follow-up data from the German population-based Gutenberg Health Study to examine whether cumulative night shift work increases the risk of incident cardiovascular disease. Among 8167 working participants, night shift workers had a higher crude incidence of cardiovascular events than day workers, but the adjusted hazard ratios for low, middle and high night shift exposure had wide confidence intervals including one.
