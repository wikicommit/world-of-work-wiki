## Summary

A 2024 discussion paper in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health reviewing over 220 articles on working hours and health published in the journal over 50 years. It finds a shift from small experimental studies to large register-based epidemiological studies, and concludes that shift work and long working hours are major occupational health risks that increase the risk of poor sleep, fatigue, sickness absence, injuries and chronic conditions such as cardiovascular disease and cancer.
