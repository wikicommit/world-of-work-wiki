## Summary

A 2025 systematic review and meta-analysis in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health of 14 studies (16 articles, 191 320 workers) comparing night-shift workers with non-shift workers for susceptibility to infectious diseases. Night-shift work was associated with a higher risk of SARS-CoV-2 infection but not with a significantly increased risk of common respiratory infections, and the certainty of the evidence was graded very low.
