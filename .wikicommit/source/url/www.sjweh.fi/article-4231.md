## Summary

A 2025 article in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health that uses three waves (2014-2018) of the Swedish Longitudinal Occupational Survey of Health (SLOSH) cohort to examine gender differences in how work-to-family and family-to-work conflict relate to depressive symptoms, by intensity of childcare. Within-person increases in both directions of conflict were associated with more depressive symptoms in men and women, with larger effects among those with heavier childcare responsibilities.
