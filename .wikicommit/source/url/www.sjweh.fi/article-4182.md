## Summary

A 2024 systematic review and meta-analysis in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health of 28 articles on the association of long working hours and shift work with suicidal ideation. The pooled odds ratios were 1.44 for long working hours and 1.34 for shift work, with higher risk for those working more than 55 hours a week than for those working fewer than 40, and for fixed night shift workers compared with non-shift workers.
