## Summary

A 2024 editorial in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health reviewing research on working hours and cardiovascular disease, from the historical struggle for the eight-hour day to recent studies. It summarises evidence on long working hours and ischaemic heart disease and stroke, including the debate over the WHO/ILO joint estimates and possible effect modification by socioeconomic position, and evidence on shift and night shift work, and outlines challenges for future research.
