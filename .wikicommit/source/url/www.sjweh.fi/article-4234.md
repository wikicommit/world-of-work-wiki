## Summary

A 2025 original article in the Scand J Work Environ Health that assessed how telework conditions during the COVID-19 pandemic related to stress among 294 Swedish white-collar workers, distinguishing the allowance to telework (how far employees could decide whether to telework) from the utilization of that allowance, and measuring perceived stress with the Single Item Stress Question and physiological stress with three days of heart rate variability during work, leisure and sleep. High allowance was associated with higher heart rate variability (lower stress), while high utilization was associated with higher perceived stress and lower heart rate variability; after adjustment for age, sex, body mass index and measured physical activity, only the association between high allowance and higher RMSSD remained.
