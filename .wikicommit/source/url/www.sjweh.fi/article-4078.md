## Summary

A 2023 cross-sectional study in the Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health of a nationally representative Norwegian sample (N=4329) examining how office design and access to telework from home relate to self-certified sickness absence. Employees in conventional open-plan offices had higher odds of sickness absence than those in private offices, access to telework from home was associated with lower odds overall, but among employees with such access the difference between open-plan and private offices was amplified.
