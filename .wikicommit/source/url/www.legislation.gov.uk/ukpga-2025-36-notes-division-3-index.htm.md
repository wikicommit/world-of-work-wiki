## Summary

The "Policy background" section of the Explanatory Notes to the UK Employment Rights Act 2025, written by the responsible government department to explain what the Act sets out to achieve under the Government's Plan to Make Work Pay. It outlines the Act's measures on one-sided flexibility (guaranteed hours, notice of shifts and payment for cancelled shifts), family-friendly rights, dismissal (including a six-month unfair dismissal qualifying period and restrictions on "fire and rehire"), equality at work, fair pay and conditions (including changes to Statutory Sick Pay), trade union law, and enforcement through a new Fair Work Agency.
