## Summary

IAB Research Report 2|2021, produced within the BIBB-IAB Qualification and Occupational Projections (QuBe project), models a "post-coronavirus scenario" for Germany up to 2040 in which ways of living, producing and working adopted during the COVID-19 pandemic persist — more working from home and video conferencing, faster growth of online retail, better labour-market matching, more diversified supply chains and stockholding, more domestic production of medicines, and changes in meat processing and aviation. Compared with the QuBe baseline projection, the scenario yields lower GDP in the medium term but slightly higher GDP by 2040, around 200,000 fewer employed persons in 2025 (210,000 fewer in 2040), and job losses concentrated in retail, business services and hospitality, with higher labour demand in public administration and IT services.
