## Summary

An IAB brief report (IAB-Kurzbericht 7/2023) examining how the employment and living situations of 60- to 70-year-old employees in Germany changed at the start of the COVID-19 pandemic, using administrative Integrated Employment Biographies (IEB) data and the IAB-HOPP online panel. It finds that employment of this age group remained stable during the first lockdown, that older employees — especially those in marginal employment (minijobs) — somewhat less often had the option to work from home, but that those who had it used it more often and for more weekly hours than before the pandemic, and that working from home was not related to worries about their own health.
