## Summary

IAB Research Report 16|2023 uses Socio-Economic Panel (SOEP) data for 1985–2021 to compare the weekly hours employees in Germany actually work with the hours they would like to work, by sex, employment type, age group and hours band. It finds that the desired hours of full-time and part-time employees have been remarkably stable over decades, with a recent trend among full-time employees towards somewhat shorter desired hours that the pandemic reinforced, persistent wishes for longer hours among part-time and marginally employed workers, and no evidence of a "quiet quitting" trend; it concludes that preferences point less to a general four-day week than to more differentiated working-time arrangements adapted to each phase of life.
