## Summary

IAB-Stellungnahme 7/2018 is the IAB's written statement for a public hearing of the North Rhine-Westphalia state parliament's labour committee on 2 May 2018 on an SPD motion concerning Germany's 1994 Working Hours Act and its role in regulating flexible working hours. Drawing on employee and establishment surveys, it describes how widespread working-time forms that deviate from the "normal working day" are, discusses the blurring of work and private life through constant availability via modern communication media, and reports research finding that working-time flexibility in Germany is primarily employer-oriented. It concludes that the existing act already allows extensive flexibility through collective agreements, which protect employees against the power asymmetry between establishments and individual workers, and that new compromises can be negotiated within that framework.
