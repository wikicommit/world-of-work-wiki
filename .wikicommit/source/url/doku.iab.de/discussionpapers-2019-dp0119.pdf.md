## Summary

IAB Discussion Paper 1/2019 by Gerhard Krug, Kirsten Kemna and Katja Hartosch examines how flexible working hours affect the health of employees in Germany, using fixed-effects panel regressions on the 2003–2011 waves of the Socio-Economic Panel (SOEP). It distinguishes employer-controlled flexibility, partially self-determined flextime with a working-time account, and fully self-determined working time, and measures health by self-rated health, days of reported sick leave and serious worries about one's health. It finds neutral or only slightly negative effects of small magnitude, with men's self-rated health more affected by self-determined flexibility and women's by employer-controlled flexibility.
