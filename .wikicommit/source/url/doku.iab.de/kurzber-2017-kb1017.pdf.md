## Summary

IAB-Kurzbericht 10/2017 uses wave 5 (2011) of the IAB's "Labour Market and Social Security" (PASS) panel to compare the actual and desired weekly working hours of 18- to 60-year-old women and men in Germany by household situation, and to examine women's views on the age of a child from which a mother can work part-time or full-time and from which a child can be cared for outside the family. It finds that women's employment and desired hours vary strongly with partner and children while men's hardly do — women would on average like about 28 hours a week and men about 35 — and that women on average see a mother returning to part-time work 3 years and 2 months, and to full-time work 7 years, after a birth, with earlier ages named by mothers of young children, East German, younger and more highly educated women.
