## Summary

IAB-Kurzbericht 24/2025 uses the IAB Establishment Panel to document the gender leadership gap in private-sector establishments in Germany: in 2024 women held 29% of top-level management positions and 42% of second-level positions, against a 45% share of all employees, with better representation in East Germany and large differences by establishment size and industry. It also finds that the share of establishments offering at least one measure to reconcile work and family rose from 32% in 2016 to 59% in 2024, mainly family-friendly working hours, and that the share of women in top management rose more strongly by 2024 in industries where such measures were already widespread in 2016.
