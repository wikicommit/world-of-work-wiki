## Summary

An IAB brief report (IAB-Kurzbericht 4/2026) analysing, with Panel Labour Market and Social Security (PASS) data for 2018–2024, how five dimensions of employment quality — pay satisfaction, work autonomy, career prospects, workload and work–life balance — developed before, during and after the COVID-19 pandemic in Germany, distinguishing employees in systemically relevant occupations from others. It finds that employment quality stayed largely stable overall, that pay satisfaction fell for all employees and stayed below its pre-crisis level, that workload rose clearly for systemically relevant employees while others were temporarily relieved, and that work–life balance improved slightly for both groups, among systemically relevant employees only for a small subgroup.
