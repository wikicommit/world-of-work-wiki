## Summary

The Health and Safety Executive's summary statistics for Great Britain 2025 report that 1.9 million workers were suffering from work-related ill health in 2024/25, including 964,000 with work-related stress, depression or anxiety and 511,000 with work-related musculoskeletal disorders, while 124 workers were killed in work-related accidents and 0.7 million sustained a non-fatal workplace injury. It estimates 40.1 million working days lost and annual costs of £22.9 billion from workplace injury and new cases of work-related ill health, compares rates across industries and with other European countries, and describes its data sources, including the Labour Force Survey and RIDDOR.
