## Summary

The Health and Safety Executive's annual Accredited Official Statistics report on work-related stress, depression or anxiety in Great Britain, published on 20 November 2025 with data up to March 2025. Based mainly on Labour Force Survey self-reports, it estimates that 964,000 workers were suffering from work-related stress, depression or anxiety in 2024/25 (409,000 new cases), accounting for 52% of work-related ill health and 22.1 million lost working days, and breaks the figures down by trend, industry, occupation, age and gender, workplace size and cause, with supporting data from the THOR-GP general-practitioner surveillance scheme.
