## Summary

The Health and Safety Executive's annual Accredited Official Statistics report on work-related musculoskeletal disorders in Great Britain, published on 20 November 2025 with data up to March 2025. Based mainly on self-reports in the Labour Force Survey, it estimates that 511,000 workers were suffering from a work-related musculoskeletal disorder in 2024/25 (173,000 new cases), causing 7.1 million lost working days, and breaks the figures down by affected body area, trend, industry, occupation, age and gender, workplace size and cause, with supporting data from the THOR-GP general-practitioner surveillance scheme.
