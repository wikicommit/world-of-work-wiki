## Summary

This Health and Safety Executive (HSE) guidance page gives shift workers practical hints and tips, drawn from commonly available advice and best practice, to improve sleep quality, increase alertness and reduce health risks. It covers driving to and from work, finding a suitable sleep schedule and a favourable sleep environment, techniques to promote sleep, diet, the use of stimulants and sedatives, physical fitness, keeping in touch with family and friends, and ways to stay alert at work.
