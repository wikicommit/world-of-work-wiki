## Summary

The results report of Argentina's second National Survey of Workers on Employment, Work, Health and Safety Conditions (ECETSS 2018), published in October 2019 under the editorship of the Superintendencia de Riesgos del Trabajo. Based on 8,966 employed people interviewed in their homes in 136 urban localities between August and December 2018, it describes employment conditions (including a 42.8% informality rate, multiple job-holding and working time), exposure to environmental, ergonomic and psychosocial risks and workplace violence, prevention resources, training and labour relations, workers' health, and gender inequalities such as unpaid domestic work and 'double presence'.
