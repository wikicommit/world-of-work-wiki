## Summary

A Statistics Denmark analysis (No. 2017:17, published 29 November 2017) asking whether Danish working time is low. It finds that average weekly hours per employed person are low by EU standards partly because many students work, but that measured per person in the population Danish working time is mid-field and slightly above the EU average because employment rates are high; it also compares working time by age group and by level of education.
