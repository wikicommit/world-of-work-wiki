## Summary

The first quarterly report of Argentina's National Directorate of Economy, Equality and Gender (Ministry of Economy) sets out gender gaps in labour-market participation, employment, unemployment, underemployment, informality, income and unpaid domestic and care work in the fourth quarter of 2021, when women's activity (50.3%) and employment (46.4%) rates reached their highest levels since 2003 while the gaps persisted. A special section examines paid domestic work in private households — the most feminised, most informal and lowest-paid sector of the economy, which lost hundreds of thousands of jobs in the pandemic — and the Registradas programme that subsidises the formal registration of these workers.
