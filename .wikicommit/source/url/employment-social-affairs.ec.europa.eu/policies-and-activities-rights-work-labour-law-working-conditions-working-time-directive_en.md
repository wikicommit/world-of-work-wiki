## Summary

The European Commission's page on the Working Time Directive (2003/88/EC) explains that the Directive requires EU Member States to guarantee all workers a 48-hour average limit on weekly working time, rest breaks, minimum daily and weekly rest, at least four weeks of paid annual leave and extra protection for night workers, while allowing sectoral derogations and an individual opt-out under strict conditions. It also describes the Commission's 2023 Interpretative Communication and Implementation Report, adopted to improve legal certainty and the Directive's implementation.
