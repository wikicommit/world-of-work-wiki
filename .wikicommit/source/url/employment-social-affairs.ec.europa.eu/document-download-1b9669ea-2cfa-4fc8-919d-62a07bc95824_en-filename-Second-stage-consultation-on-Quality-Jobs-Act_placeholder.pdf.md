## Summary

A European Commission consultation document of 20 July 2026 (C(2026) 5109 final) launching the second-phase consultation of EU social partners under Article 154 TFEU on a Quality Jobs Act. It summarises the first-phase replies of trade unions and employer organisations and sets out challenges and possible avenues for EU action on algorithmic management and AI at work, occupational safety and health (including the Workplace and Display Screen Equipment Directives), workers' rights in subcontracting chains, just transitions, enforcement and the role of social partners, and simplification.
