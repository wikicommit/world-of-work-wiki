## Summary

The WHO guidelines on mental health at work (2022) give evidence-based global recommendations on organizational interventions, manager and worker training, individual interventions, return to work after absence associated with mental health conditions, and gaining employment for people living with mental health conditions, graded by strength and certainty of evidence under the GRADE approach. They set out ten categories of psychosocial risks at work, strongly recommend manager training for mental health, reasonable work accommodations and supported employment, and make no recommendation for or against screening programmes during employment.
