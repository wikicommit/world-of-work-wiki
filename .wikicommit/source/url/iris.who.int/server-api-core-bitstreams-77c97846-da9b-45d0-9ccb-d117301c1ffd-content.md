## Summary

Healthy and safe telework, a 2021 technical brief by the World Health Organization and the International Labour Organization, reviews the evidence on the physical and mental health impacts of telework and gives practical advice on organizing it to protect and promote workers' health, safety and well-being. It covers ergonomics and the home work environment, psychosocial risks such as isolation, long hours and cyberbullying, health behaviours, the roles of governments, employers and workers under ILO occupational safety and health standards, and the role of occupational health services.
