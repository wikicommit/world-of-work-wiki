## Summary

This 2024 Statistics Finland review of the Quality of Work Life Survey 2023 reports that 35 per cent of Finnish wage and salary earners worked remotely in 2023 and 22 per cent did so for at least half of their working hours, with home increasingly the main place of work. It compares women and men, employer sectors and socio-economic groups on the share of remote work, the ability to influence it, workplace-presence requirements, recording of remote working hours, ergonomic and other conditions of the remote work environment, and interest in remote work among those who cannot do it.
