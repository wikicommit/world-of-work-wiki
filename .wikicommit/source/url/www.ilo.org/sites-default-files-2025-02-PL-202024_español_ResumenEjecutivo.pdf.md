## Summary

The executive summary of the ILO's Panorama Laboral 2024 for Latin America and the Caribbean finds relative stability in the region's main labour indicators in 2024: the employment rate rose to 58.9%, participation held at 62.7% and unemployment fell to 6.1%, although neither participation nor employment has substantially exceeded 2012 levels. Informality eased slightly to 47.6% and real wages improved modestly, but gaps by sex, age, country and urban/rural area persist, youth unemployment (13.8%) is almost three times the adult rate, and unemployment is projected at 5.8–6.2% in 2025; special chapters address the gender wage gap and youth employment.
