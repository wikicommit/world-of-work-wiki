## Summary

The ILO's publication page for the Strategy for the Promotion of Formalization in Latin America and the Caribbean (2024-2030), known as FORLAC 2.0, dated 21 May 2024. It explains that half of all working people in the region work in the informal economy, a structural and persistent problem concentrated among women, youth, certain ethnic groups, migrants and other groups, recalls the first FORLAC programme implemented by the ILO Regional Office in 2013–2017, and outlines the contents of the relaunched regional formalisation strategy planned over ILO budget cycles from 2024 to 2030.
