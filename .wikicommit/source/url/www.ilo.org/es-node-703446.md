## Summary

An ILO press release of 21 April 2025 presents a report by the ILO Regional Office for Latin America and the Caribbean on a survey of more than a thousand workers on web-based digital labour platforms in 21 countries of the region. It reports that these workers are mostly urban, young and highly educated, often work for clients outside the region, mainly in the United States and Canada, earn very uneven hourly incomes (median USD 2.57, average USD 5.48), and for 52 per cent do not rely on platform work as their main income, while close to 40 per cent lack health and social security coverage. The ILO links the report to the International Labour Conference's June 2025 discussion of platform work.
