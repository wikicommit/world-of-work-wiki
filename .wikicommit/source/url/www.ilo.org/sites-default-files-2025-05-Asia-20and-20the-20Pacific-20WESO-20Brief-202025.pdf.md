## Summary

This ILO brief, the May 2025 Asia and the Pacific update of the World Employment and Social Outlook, reports that geopolitical tensions and trade disruptions have moderately worsened the region's employment outlook: employment growth for 2025 has been revised down from 1.9% (38 million jobs) to 1.7% (34 million), still the highest of any ILO region. It estimates that about 2.9% of the region's employment — a little more than 55 million jobs, almost half in manufacturing — is linked to final demand in the United States, notes that around 1.3 billion workers (about 66%) are informal, and calls for social protection, active labour market policies and support for enterprises.
