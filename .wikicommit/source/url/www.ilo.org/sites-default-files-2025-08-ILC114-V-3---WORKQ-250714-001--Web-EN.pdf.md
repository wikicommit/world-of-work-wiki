## Summary

Report V(3) to the 114th Session (2026) of the International Labour Conference transmits to governments the International Labour Office's draft texts of a Convention and a Recommendation on decent work in the platform economy, prepared after the Conference's first discussion of the standard-setting item in June 2025. The Office commentary explains the drafts and invites constituents' views on proposed changes, such as the ambiguity of "organizes and/or facilitates work" in the definition of a digital labour platform, the meaning of "remuneration" or "payment", and moving some provisions from the Convention to the Recommendation, while the draft texts cover definitions, scope, occupational safety and health, employment classification, remuneration, working time, social security, automated systems, personal data and dispute resolution for digital platform workers.
