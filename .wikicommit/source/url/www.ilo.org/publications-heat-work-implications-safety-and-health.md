## Summary

This is the ILO's publication page for "Heat at work: Implications for safety and health" (25 July 2024), a report on how heat stress — aggravated by higher daily temperatures and more frequent and severe heatwaves — harms workers' safety and health, from heat exhaustion, heatstroke and death to chronic cardiovascular, respiratory and kidney disease. It notes that national legal provisions on excessive heat are mostly general and do not adequately address climate change-related dangers, and that the report analyses heat-stress legislation in 21 countries to identify common provisions for workplace-level measures.
