## Summary

Report V(4) to the 114th Session (2026) of the International Labour Conference, published by the International Labour Office in March 2026, is the fourth and final report in the double-discussion procedure on decent work in the platform economy. It summarizes the replies of 74 governments, 106 employers' organizations and 131 workers' organizations to the draft texts in Report V(3), with an Office commentary on each provision, and presents revised draft texts of a Convention and a Recommendation for the second discussion in June 2026. Responding to calls for a principles-based, streamlined instrument, the Office cut the drafts from 31 Articles and 35 Paragraphs to 24 Articles and 19 Paragraphs, left the text agreed by the Committee in 2025 essentially unchanged, removed cross-references to other international labour standards, and restructured the remuneration provisions to distinguish digital platform workers in an employment relationship from those who are not.
