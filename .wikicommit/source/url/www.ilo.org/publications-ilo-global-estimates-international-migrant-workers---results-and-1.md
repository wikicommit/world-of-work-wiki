## Summary

This is the ILO's publication page for the third edition of "ILO Global Estimates on International Migrant Workers – Results and Methodology" (30 June 2021), a report that presents estimates of the stock of international migrant workers in 2019, broken down by age, sex, country-income group and region, together with the estimation methodology. The estimates predate the COVID-19 crisis and are offered as a benchmark for analysing its effects on labour migration, and the periodic report is presented as contributing to the Sustainable Development Goals and to policymaking at country, regional and global levels.
