## Summary

Realizing decent work in the platform economy (ILC.113/Report V(1), 2024) is the International Labour Office's law and practice report for the first discussion of a standard-setting item on decent work in the platform economy at the 113th Session (2025) of the International Labour Conference. It describes the growth of platforms that connect businesses and clients to workers and the characteristics of work on them, examines how existing international labour standards apply to platform workers and where gaps lie, and reviews national legislation, case law and collective agreements on employment classification, remuneration, working time, deactivation, personal data, dispute resolution, social security, representation, algorithmic transparency and compliance. It concludes that a new standard covering both location-based and online platforms, and both employees and self-employed workers, is needed, and appends a questionnaire to governments.
