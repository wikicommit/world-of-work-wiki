## Summary

An ILO press release of 17 April 2025 presents ILO Southern Cone Technical Report No. 50, "Trabajo, empleo, protección laboral y social en América Latina y el Caribe, 1994–2024", which reviews three decades of labour market change in Latin America and the Caribbean. The report finds substantial progress in job quality, labour inclusion, social protection and labour institutions since the mid-1990s, but persistent productivity gaps, informality affecting about half of workers, and obstacles for vulnerable young people and women, alongside new challenges from technological, climate and demographic transitions. It recommends integrated productive and employment policies, formalisation measures, stronger tripartite social dialogue and inclusive working lives for young people, women and older people.
