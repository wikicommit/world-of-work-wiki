## Summary

This June 2014 report by the U.S. Council of Economic Advisers sets out nine facts about American families and work: mothers are increasingly breadwinners and fathers increasingly caregivers, women make up nearly half of the labour force and a growing share of its most skilled workers, most children live in households where all parents work, and eldercare is a growing responsibility of workers. It argues that men and women alike face work–family conflict, that many workplaces have not kept up with these changes in flexibility and paid leave, and that providing workplace flexibility and paid leave strengthens families, businesses and the economy.
