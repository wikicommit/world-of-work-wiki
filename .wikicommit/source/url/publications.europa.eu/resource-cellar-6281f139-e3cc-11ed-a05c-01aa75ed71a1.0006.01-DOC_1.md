## Summary

The European Commission's 2023 Interpretative Communication on the Working Time Directive 2003/88/EC (2023/C 143/06, a corrected text that replaces the version published on 24 March 2023) brings together in one document the Directive's provisions and more than 80 judgments and orders of the Court of Justice of the EU interpreting them, covering scope, definitions, minimum rest periods, the 48-hour average weekly limit, paid annual leave, night and shift work, and derogations. It updates the 2017 Communication with over 30 later rulings — notably on when stand-by time counts as working time, the obligation to record each worker's daily working time, concurrent contracts and military personnel — and marks separately the points on which the Commission gives its own, non-binding view.
