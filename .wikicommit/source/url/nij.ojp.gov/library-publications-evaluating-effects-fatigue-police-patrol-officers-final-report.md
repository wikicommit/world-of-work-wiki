## Summary

This National Institute of Justice catalogue page describes "Evaluating the Effects of Fatigue on Police Patrol Officers: Final Report" (NCJ 184188), a 122-page NIJ Research Report published in February 2000 that evaluates the prevalence and effects of objectively measured fatigue among a sample of patrol officers. Its abstract reports that officers who routinely worked more consecutive hours than would be legal in other public service industries showed fatigue levels six times higher than those of shift workers in industrial and mining jobs, that only 26 percent of officers reported averaging seven or more hours of sleep a day, and that the report offers guidelines for reducing fatigue or ameliorating its effects.
