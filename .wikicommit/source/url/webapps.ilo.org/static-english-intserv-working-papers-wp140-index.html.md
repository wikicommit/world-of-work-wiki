## Summary

An ILO working paper, produced with Poland's NASK National Research Institute, that updates the ILO's 2023 Global Index of Occupational Exposure to Generative AI. Combining a survey of 1,640 workers in Poland scoring tasks from the Polish 6-digit occupational classification, expert validation and an AI model that predicts task scores for ISCO-08, it classifies occupations into four exposure gradients and estimates that about one in four workers worldwide (24%) are in an occupation with some GenAI exposure and 3.3% in the highest gradient, with higher exposure for women and in high-income countries, while stressing that exposure signals likely job transformation rather than automation of whole occupations.
