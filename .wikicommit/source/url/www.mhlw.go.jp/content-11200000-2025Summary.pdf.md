## Summary

This is the English summary of Japan's 2025 White Paper on Measures to Prevent Karoshi, etc., the tenth edition of the statutory annual report to the Diet under the Act Promoting Measures to Prevent Death and Injury from Overwork, prepared by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. It reports indicators on working hours, annual paid leave, the work-interval system and workplace mental health measures, analyses the rise in workers' compensation claims and approvals for cerebrovascular and cardiovascular diseases and especially mental disorders through FY2024, presents survey results for priority industries including the food service industry, and outlines the measures the government is taking to prevent karoshi.
