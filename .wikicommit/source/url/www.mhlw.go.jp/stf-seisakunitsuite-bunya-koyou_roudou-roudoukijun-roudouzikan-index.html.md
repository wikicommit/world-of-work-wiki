## Summary

A Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (Japan) policy page on working hours and days off that sets out the main statutory rules: the 8-hour day and 40-hour week, minimum breaks and weekly days off, and the labour–management agreement under Article 36 of the Labour Standards Act that permits overtime and holiday work. It also outlines variable working hours systems, the flextime system, deemed working hours systems (including the two discretionary work systems) and the minimum entitlement to annual paid leave, and links to related measures such as the cap on overtime and guidelines on recording working time.
