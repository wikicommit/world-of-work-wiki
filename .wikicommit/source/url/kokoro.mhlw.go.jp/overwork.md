## Summary

A page of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's workers' mental health portal "Kokoro no Mimi" summarising Japan's measures against overwork. It quotes the statutory definition of karoshi etc. in Article 2 of the Act on Promotion of Measures to Prevent Karoshi etc., describes the government's framework under that Act (the outline of measures, the council, the annual white paper, research and the November awareness month) and the employers' comprehensive measures to prevent health impairment from overwork, and lists a timeline of overwork-related policy from 1947 to 2023, including successive revisions of the workers' compensation recognition criteria for cerebrovascular and heart diseases.
