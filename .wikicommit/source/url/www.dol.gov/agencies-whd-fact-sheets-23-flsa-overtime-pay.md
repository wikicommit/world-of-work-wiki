## Summary

This Wage and Hour Division fact sheet summarises the overtime pay requirements of the Fair Labor Standards Act: unless exempt, covered employees must be paid at least one and one-half times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek. It explains the fixed 168-hour workweek, how the regular rate is computed and what it excludes, and common compliance problems such as flat sums for overtime, fixed salaries for long workweeks and attempts to waive overtime.
