## Summary

The US Department of Labor's Wage and Hour Division fact sheet explains what counts as compensable "hours worked" under the Fair Labor Standards Act, starting from the statutory definition of "employ" as "to suffer or permit to work" and the concept of the workday. It applies these principles to waiting time, on-call time, rest and meal periods, sleeping time, training and travel, and notes typical problems such as unrecorded work done at the desk during lunch.
