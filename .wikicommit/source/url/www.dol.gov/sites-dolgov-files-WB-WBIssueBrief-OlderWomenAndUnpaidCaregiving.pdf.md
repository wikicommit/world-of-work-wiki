## Summary

A November 2023 issue brief from the U.S. Department of Labor's Women's Bureau on the unpaid care that women aged 55 and older provide to children, adults and older relatives. Using pooled American Time Use Survey data for 2017–2019 and 2021–2022, it finds that on an average day 23.5% of these women provide unpaid care, for about 2 hours 11 minutes, and that they account for 35.3% of all unpaid eldercare providers; it also uses 2021 American Community Survey data to describe the 1.32 million grandmothers raising grandchildren. It argues that this care underpins the U.S. labour force while reducing caregivers' work hours, earnings and retirement income.
