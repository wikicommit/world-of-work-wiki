## Summary

An Istat press release of 25 February 2026, based on the 2023 permanent population census, reports that just under 3.4 million employed people in Italy (13.8%) worked remotely at some point in the four weeks before 1 October 2023, 5.9% of them for at least half their working days, a share stable since 2022 after a pandemic peak of 15.1% in 2021 and only 4.8% in 2018-2019. Italy's 5.9% share of people usually working from home remained well below the EU average of 9.1% in 2023. Remote work is most common in the Centre-North and large cities, led by Lazio (21.5%) and Lombardy (18.6%), and least common in the South and the Islands.
