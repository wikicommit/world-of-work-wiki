## Summary

The opening page of Module 2 of NIOSH's online training for nurses on shift work and long work hours sets out how these work schedules raise health and safety risks. It explains that they disturb sleep and circadian rhythms and reduce time for family and other responsibilities, and that long hours also increase exposure to workplace hazards and shorten recovery time. These immediate effects promote stress, fatigue and poor health behaviours that can lead to illness and injury, with consequences that extend to families, employers, patients and the wider community.
