## Summary

This Statistics Netherlands (CBS) Statistische Trends article, published on 3 October 2025, follows 6.9 million employees who had a job in the fourth quarter of 2021 to see how their contractual working hours had changed two years later, comparing permanent and flexible contracts, men and women, age groups and job sizes. Most employees (53%) kept the same number of hours; those who changed hours often ended up in a larger part-time job of 28 to 32 hours, varying by sector; employees under 25 were the most flexible in their hours; and women in every age group were more likely than men of the same age both to increase and to decrease their hours.
