## Summary

A Statistics Netherlands (CBS) news release of 12 April 2023 on the first nine years in the labour market of men and women who left education with an mbo, hbo or university diploma in 2007–2009. It finds that women work part-time twice as often as men one year after graduating (30% against 14% of employees), that the gap widens over the following years, that full-time working women switch to part-time more often than men — especially when cohabiting — and that the switch peaks for women in the year after the birth of their first child, while most men keep working full time.
