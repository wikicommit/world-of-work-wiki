## Summary

This Statistics Netherlands (CBS) Statistische Trends article, published on 20 August 2025, describes how much employees sit on an average working day, both at work and in their free time. In 2024 employees sat on average 4.5 hours a working day at work, 1.1 hours while commuting and 3.3 hours in their free time, with only free-time sitting rising slightly in recent years (mainly between 2017 and 2022); sitting at work varies by occupation and is associated with working from home, screen work and part-time or full-time work, free-time sitting is associated with age, and there is no pronounced link between sitting at work and sitting in free time.
