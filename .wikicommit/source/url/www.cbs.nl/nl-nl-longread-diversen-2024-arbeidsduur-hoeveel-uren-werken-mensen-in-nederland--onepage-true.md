## Summary

A Statistics Netherlands (CBS) publication from February 2024 explaining why Dutch figures on working hours differ depending on the definition used (contractual, usual or actual hours), the data source (the Labour Force Survey, EBB, or the Polis administration of payroll records) and the target population. After aligning the populations of the two sources for 2022, it finds that usual hours in the EBB and base hours in the Polis administration agree closely both on average and in distribution, while actual hours agree on average but not in distribution because leave and sickness are treated differently; the Labour Accounts' quarterly figures, which correct register hours for leave, sickness and unpaid overtime, closely match the EBB.
