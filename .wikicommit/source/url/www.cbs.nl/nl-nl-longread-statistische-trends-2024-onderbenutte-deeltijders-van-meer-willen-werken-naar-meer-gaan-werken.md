## Summary

This Statistics Netherlands (CBS) Statistische Trends article, published on 10 October 2024, uses new Labour Force Survey (EBB) figures to examine underemployed part-time workers — people who want and are able to work more hours but do not — their characteristics, how many actually go on to work more hours, and their contribution to growth in total working hours. In 2023, 48% of underemployed part-time workers were in education, an average of 29% worked more hours a quarter later (against 7% of the rest of the employed labour force), and their contribution to growth in total working hours was limited, with unemployed people contributing more than four times as many full-time equivalents per quarter.
