## Summary

A February 2017 blog post on the Statistics Estonia website, written by a Tallinn University sociologist, asks whether flexible working time — part-time work, work at atypical hours (evenings, nights, weekends) and working from home — is used in Estonia to reconcile work and family life, drawing on the 2015 Estonian Labour Force Survey and an equality monitoring survey. It finds that households with and without children use these forms of flexibility alike, that their use is explained more by occupation than by family status, and suggests that atypical hours may strain rather than ease families' time use.
