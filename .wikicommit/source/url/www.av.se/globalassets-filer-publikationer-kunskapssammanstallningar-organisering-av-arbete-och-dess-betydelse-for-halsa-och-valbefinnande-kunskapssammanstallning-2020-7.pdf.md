## Summary

A 2020 knowledge compilation (Kunskapssammanställning 2020:7) published by the Swedish Agency for Work Environment Expertise (Myndigheten för arbetsmiljökunskap) under a government assignment, reviewing 80 peer-reviewed publications from 2009–2019 on how the organisation of work affects employee health and well-being in Swedish and Nordic contexts. It identifies two main themes — conflicting logics in the public sector under New Public Management, and the temporal and spatial organisation of work through employment forms and flexibility — and concludes with knowledge gaps and general advice to employers, such as giving employees influence over their work situation and addressing the use of temporary and agency employment.
