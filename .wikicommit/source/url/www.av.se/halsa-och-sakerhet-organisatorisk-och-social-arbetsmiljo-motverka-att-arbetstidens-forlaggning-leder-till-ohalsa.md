## Summary

A Swedish Work Environment Authority (Arbetsmiljöverket) guidance page explaining that long shifts of 10 hours or more, long working weeks of 50 hours or more, night work and shift work carry risks of ill health such as sleep disturbance, depression, cardiovascular disease and cancer. It tells employers to find out how working time is actually distributed, to risk-assess and counter long shifts, constant availability and split shifts with measures such as changed staffing, moving tasks from night to day and more breaks, and stresses that this duty applies even when the Working Hours Act is complied with.
