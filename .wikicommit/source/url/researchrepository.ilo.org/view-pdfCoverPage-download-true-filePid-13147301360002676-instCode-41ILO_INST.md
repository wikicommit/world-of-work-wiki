## Summary

The executive summary of the ILO's Employment and Social Trends 2026 (World of Work Series), subtitled 'Steadiness amid high uncertainty'. It finds that progress in employment quality has stalled — extreme working poverty fell by only 3.1 percentage points between 2015 and 2025 and informality rose by 0.3 points — while the global unemployment rate stayed at 4.9 per cent in 2025, and it discusses gender gaps, weak productivity and labour income growth, youth labour market conditions and the possible effects of AI adoption, and how trade disruptions affect employment and decent work.
