## Summary

Service Public, the French administration's official information site, explains the rules on overtime (heures supplémentaires) for private-sector employees in France: hours worked at the employer's request beyond the statutory 35-hour week or 1,607 hours a year, subject to an annual quota (220 hours per employee by default), paid at a premium of 25% for the first eight hours and 50% thereafter unless a collective agreement sets other rates of at least 10%, and giving rise to mandatory compensatory rest beyond the quota. It also covers the treatment of public holidays and paid leave in the overtime count, payslip, tax and social-contribution rules, and remedies in disputes, and explains that part-time employees instead work "complementary hours" (heures complémentaires) with their own limits, premiums and right of refusal.
