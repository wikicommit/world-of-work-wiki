## Summary

A Service Public (French government) information page, verified on 14 August 2026, on the rules for telework by employees in the French private sector. It defines telework as work that could have been done on the employer's premises but is done voluntarily elsewhere using information and communication technologies, and explains how it is set up (by collective agreement, employer charter or individual agreement), where and how often it can take place, when the employer or employee may refuse it, the employer's obligations on health and safety, equipment, data protection, workload and costs, and the teleworker's rights and obligations.
