## Summary

This EU-OSHA publication page presents the report "OSH Pulse 2025: Occupational safety and health in the era of climate and digital change" (23 September 2025, 65 pages), which sets out the findings of a survey commissioned by EU-OSHA. More than 28,000 employed workers in the EU Member States, Iceland, Norway and Switzerland were interviewed in March and April 2025 about their use of digital technologies for work, psychosocial factors, climate change-related risks and work-related health outcomes.
