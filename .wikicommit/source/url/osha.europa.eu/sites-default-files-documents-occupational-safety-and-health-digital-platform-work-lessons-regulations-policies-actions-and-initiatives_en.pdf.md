## Summary

An EU-OSHA policy brief that draws lessons for occupational safety and health (OSH) from regulations, policies and initiatives on digital platform work, based on four case studies (the Spanish Riders' Law, the Bologna Charter in its Italian context, the French legislative framework, and actions by labour and social security inspectorates in Belgium, Spain and Poland), a literature review and a survey of EU-OSHA's national focal points. It finds that few measures address OSH directly, that the classification of platform workers as self-employed is the main obstacle, and sets out four takeaways for policymakers: binding and comprehensive OSH measures, awareness raising, sharing of good practice, and more knowledge and data.
