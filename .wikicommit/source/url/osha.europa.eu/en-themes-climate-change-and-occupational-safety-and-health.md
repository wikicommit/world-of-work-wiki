## Summary

EU-OSHA's theme page on climate change and occupational safety and health explains how rising temperatures, extreme weather events and air pollution are increasing existing risks to workers or creating new ones, and notes that one third of EU workers are exposed to such risks. It identifies outdoor workers (farmers and forestry workers, construction workers, emergency services) and indoor workers in poorly ventilated or heat-intensive settings as groups at risk, and summarises policy responses and guidance such as risk assessment following the hierarchy of control, temperature limits in some countries, heat action plans and adjusted work schedules.
