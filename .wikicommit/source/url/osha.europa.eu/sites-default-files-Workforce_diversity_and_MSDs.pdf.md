## Summary

This 2020 EU-OSHA European Risk Observatory report examines whether workforce diversity is associated with poorer working conditions and higher exposure to occupational safety and health risks, focusing on musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs) among women, migrant and LGBTI workers. Combining a literature review, analysis of EU survey data (including regressions on the 2015 European Working Conditions Survey), stakeholder interviews, focus groups and nine policy case studies, it finds that all three groups report poorer health and more MSDs, are exposed to combinations of physical, organisational and psychosocial risks such as discrimination and harassment, and are shaped by occupational segregation, and it closes with policy recommendations.
