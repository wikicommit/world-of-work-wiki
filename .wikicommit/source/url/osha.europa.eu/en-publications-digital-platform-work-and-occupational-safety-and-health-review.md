## Summary

This EU-OSHA publication page presents the report "Digital platform work and occupational safety and health: a review" (13 October 2021, 43 pages), which defines and characterises digital platform work and workers and examines the occupational safety and health risks associated with it, such as digital surveillance, performance pressure and job insecurity. The report highlights that most platform workers' self-employed status often leaves them outside EU and Member State OSH regulation, and discusses new initiatives and legislation aimed at improving their working conditions.
