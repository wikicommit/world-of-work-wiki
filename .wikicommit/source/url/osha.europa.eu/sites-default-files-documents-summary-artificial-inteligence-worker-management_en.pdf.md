## Summary

The executive summary of an EU-OSHA report on artificial intelligence-based worker management (AIWM), produced within the agency's research programme on digitalisation and occupational safety and health and based on a literature review, 22 expert interviews, consultation of national focal points and ESENER-3 data. It defines AIWM, describes its uptake, its goals and its risks to workers' safety, health and privacy, reviews EU and national regulatory contexts, and makes eleven recommendations for human-centred, transparent and participatory AIWM.
