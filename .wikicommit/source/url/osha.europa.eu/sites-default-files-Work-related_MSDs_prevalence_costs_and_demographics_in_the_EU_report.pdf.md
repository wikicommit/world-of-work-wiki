## Summary

This 2019 EU-OSHA European Risk Observatory report pulls together EU-wide survey and administrative data, complemented by national sources from ten Member States, to describe how common musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs) are among EU workers, their health and economic impact, the physical, organisational and psychosocial risk factors associated with them, and how establishments prevent them. It finds that roughly three in five EU-28 workers report MSD complaints, that prevalence varies widely by country, sector, occupation, gender, age and education, and that workers with MSDs are more often absent from work. It closes with policy pointers for integrated, participatory and diversity-sensitive prevention and for more complete and consistent data on MSDs.
