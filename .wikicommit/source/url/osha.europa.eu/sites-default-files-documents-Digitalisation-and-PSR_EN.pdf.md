## Summary

This 2024 EU-OSHA report reviews over one hundred EU-OSHA publications, together with headline findings from the OSH Pulse 2022 and ESENER 2019 surveys, to identify the psychosocial risk factors associated with digital technologies at work. It analyses five areas — advanced robotics and AI, smart digital systems, digital platform work, remote working and AI for worker management — finding recurring risks such as cognitive overload, fear of job loss, time pressure, lack of autonomy and trust, isolation and poor work-life balance, and reviews EU and national legislation and good practice before setting out policy pointers for a human-centred approach to OSH.
