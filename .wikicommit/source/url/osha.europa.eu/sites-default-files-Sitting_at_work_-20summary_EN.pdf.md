## Summary

This 2021 executive summary of an EU-OSHA report examines prolonged static sitting at work — defined as being sedentary for two hours or longer at a time — covering how much Europeans sit at work, which workers are most exposed, the health effects linked to prolonged sitting, and existing regulations and guidelines. It recommends limiting sitting at work and promoting "dynamic sitting" and movement through a prevention strategy built on ergonomics, work organisation, worker participation and health promotion, with practical examples and pointers for policy-makers on gender, age, public health and schools.
