## Summary

Spain's National Statistics Institute (INE) press release of 19 June 2026 presents results of the 2025 Labour Force Survey (EPA) module on reconciliation between work and family life: 33.4% of people aged 18–74 had care responsibilities for children, grandchildren or dependent relatives, more often women than men. It reports that 18.2% of parents used professional childcare for all their children, that almost one in four employed carers changed something at work to reconcile work and family (most often adapting their schedule), and that 40.9% of people who raised a child under 15 used neither birth leave nor childcare leave; the module was run with Eurostat under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/2312.
