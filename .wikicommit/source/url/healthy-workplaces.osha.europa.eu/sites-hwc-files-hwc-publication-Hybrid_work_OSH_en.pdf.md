## Summary

This 2023 EU-OSHA discussion paper reviews the scientific literature and expert views on hybrid work — a combination of telework and work at the employer's premises — and its implications for working conditions, management practices, wellbeing and occupational safety and health. It describes the forms and spread of hybrid work in Europe since the COVID-19 pandemic, its physical risks (sedentary behaviour, musculoskeletal disorders, digital eye strain) and psychosocial effects (work intensity, technostress, isolation, work–life balance), and sets out implications for risk assessment, prevention, regulation, compensation of occupational diseases and further research.
