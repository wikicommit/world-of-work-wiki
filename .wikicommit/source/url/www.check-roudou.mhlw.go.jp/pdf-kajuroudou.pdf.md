## Summary

This pamphlet for a seminar on eliminating overwork, produced under a Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare commission in fiscal year 2015, argues that long working hours and other excessive work lower employees' satisfaction, raise the risk of cerebrovascular and heart disease and mental disorders, and expose companies to compensation claims and loss of reputation. It sets out what employers need to know to prevent overwork — a top-level policy, a PDCA-based plan, the roles of managers, health officers and occupational physicians, the limits on overtime under Article 36 agreements, proper recording of working hours and the use of health committees — together with physician interview guidance for long-hours workers and the new Stress Check Program. It closes with six anonymised company case studies of reduced overtime and an outline of the national framework for preventing karoshi.
