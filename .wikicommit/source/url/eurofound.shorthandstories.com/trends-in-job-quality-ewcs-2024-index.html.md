## Summary

Eurofound's digital story on the first findings of the European Working Conditions Survey (EWCS) 2024 reports trends in job quality in the EU across the seven dimensions of its job quality framework: physical environment, social environment, working time quality, work intensity, skills and discretion, prospects and earnings. It finds improvements since 2010 in most dimensions — fewer long working hours, more control over working time, better career prospects — alongside rising exposure to high temperatures, persistent verbal abuse and humiliating behaviour, work intensification for women, and gender gaps in several indices.
