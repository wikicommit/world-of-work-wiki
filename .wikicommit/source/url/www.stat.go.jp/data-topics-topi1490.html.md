## Summary

Statistics Topics No. 149, published by the Statistics Bureau of Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications on 20 September 2026 ahead of Respect for the Aged Day, summarises official statistics on older people in Japan. It reports that the population aged 65 and over stands at 36.24 million, a record 29.6% of the total population and the highest share among 39 countries compared, and that the number of employed people aged 65 and over has risen for 22 consecutive years to a record 9.43 million, 13.8% of all employed people. It adds that employment rates for every age group over 65 are at record highs and high by international standards, that 76.1% of employees aged 65 and over (excluding executives) are non-regular staff, and that older workers are concentrated in wholesale and retail trade and in medical care and welfare.
