## Summary

An SSB analysis (2019/08) by Statistics Norway comparing sickness absence of at least one week, as measured by Labour Force Surveys, in Norway and six other northern European countries from 1983 to 2017. Norway has had the highest sickness absence since 2006, not because it rose in Norway but because it fell in Sweden and the Netherlands; differences in the composition of the employed explain little. The analysis concludes that sick people in Norway tend to remain on sickness benefit, while in the other countries — as after the Dutch 2002 reform — they are more exposed to becoming unemployed.
