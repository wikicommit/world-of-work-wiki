## Summary

A Statistics Norway article, last updated in April 2026, on how much immigrants in Norway work, based on register-based employment statistics for immigrants aged 20–66 in the fourth quarter of 2025. Immigrants had a lower employment rate than the rest of the population (68 versus 80 per cent) and, among those in work, a lower full-time share (74 versus 79 per cent). Both were highest for immigrants from the Nordic countries and EU/EFTA countries and lowest for those from Asia and Africa, and they also vary by sex, length of residence and occupation.
