## Summary

An INEGI press release (no. 204/24, 26 March 2024) issued for the International Day of Domestic Workers on 30 March. Using Mexico's National Survey of Occupation and Employment (ENOE) for the third quarter of 2023, it profiles the 2.5 million people aged 15 and over in paid domestic work (4.2% of all employed people, nine in ten of them women) and their working conditions: 95.0% worked informally, 97.1% had no written contract, 67.8% earned up to one minimum wage and 69.5% received no employment benefits.
