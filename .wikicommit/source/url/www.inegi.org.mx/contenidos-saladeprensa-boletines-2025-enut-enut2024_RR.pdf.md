## Summary

INEGI's results report for Mexico's 2024 National Time Use Survey (ENUT) describes how people aged 12 and over divided their week between paid work for the market, unpaid domestic, care, community and volunteer work, and personal activities such as study, leisure and personal care. It reports that the population's total work time averaged 59.6 hours a week, that women spent 21.5 more hours a week than men on unpaid domestic, care, community and volunteer work while men spent 9.1 more hours on market work, and that total work time and its gender gap both fell between 2014, 2019 and 2024.
