## Summary

A Statistics Canada Canadian Social Trends article using four cycles of the General Social Survey on time use to show that Canadian workers' time with family on a typical workday fell from about 250 minutes in 1986 to 206 minutes in 2005, while time spent alone rose. The increase in hours of paid work was the main factor behind the decline, followed by watching television alone, eating alone, less time spent at meals and watching television, and fewer social activities outside the home.
