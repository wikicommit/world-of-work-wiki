## Summary

A 2016 Statistics Canada fact sheet in the series Spotlight on Canadians: Results from the General Social Survey, examining how satisfied mothers and fathers of children aged 17 and under who work full-time were with their work-life balance, using the 2012 General Social Survey. Three in four such parents (75%) were satisfied or very satisfied, but mothers less often than fathers, and satisfaction was lower among lone parents, parents of young children, parents who were also caregivers, those working 50 or more hours a week and shift workers. Flexible schedules and access to leave without pay were associated with higher satisfaction, and satisfaction varied by province.
