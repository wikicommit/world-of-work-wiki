## Summary

A December 2018 Insights on Canadian Society article from Statistics Canada that uses the 2016 General Social Survey on Canadians at Work and Home to examine workplace harassment experienced in the past year by employees aged 15 to 64. It reports that 19% of women and 13% of men had been harassed at work — most often through verbal abuse — that women were far more likely to report sexual harassment (4% versus less than 1%), mostly by clients or customers, and that workers in health occupations and unionized jobs reported the highest rates. Harassment was associated with lower job satisfaction, motivation and sense of belonging, poorer mental health and more stress, and the workplace associations were strongest when the harasser was a supervisor or manager.
