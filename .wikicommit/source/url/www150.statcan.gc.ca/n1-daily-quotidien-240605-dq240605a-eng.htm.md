## Summary

This Statistics Canada release in The Daily (5 June 2024) presents highlights from the 2022 Time Use Survey, including a new study comparing the time use and well-being of employees who worked from home on their diary day, teleworkers who worked on-site that day, and non-teleworkers. After accounting for socioeconomic and job characteristics, working from home saved more than an hour of commuting without changing paid work time, and was associated with more time on unpaid housework, childcare, sleep and leisure, less time on personal care, and greater satisfaction with work-life balance. Teleworkers who worked on-site that day reported the most time pressure.
