## Summary

This chapter of Statistics Canada's Women in Canada: A Gender-based Statistical Report (2018) uses the 1986, 2010 and 2015 General Social Surveys on Time Use and the 2012 General Social Survey on Caregiving and Care Receiving to examine how women and men aged 25 to 54 divide their time between paid work, housework, caregiving and leisure. It finds that their total work burden of paid and unpaid work was equivalent in 2015 when only primary activities are counted, but that women's was 1.2 hours a day greater when unpaid work done simultaneously with other activities is included. Over 30 years women's housework time fell and men's rose, both sexes spent more time on child care, and women remained the main caregivers, had less leisure, and reported more time pressure than men.
