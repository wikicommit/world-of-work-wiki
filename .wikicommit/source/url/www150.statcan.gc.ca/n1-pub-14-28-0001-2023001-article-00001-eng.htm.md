## Summary

A Statistics Canada Quality of Employment in Canada indicator article on average weekly working hours, using Labour Force Survey data from 1976 to 2022. Average usual hours in the main job fell from 38.6 to 35.8 a week over the period, partly because more women and students were employed; men's hours declined but remained longer than women's. The article also gives a 2022 snapshot of full-time employment and of hours by industry, occupation and self-employment, and explains the 1997 change in how the survey measures usual hours.
