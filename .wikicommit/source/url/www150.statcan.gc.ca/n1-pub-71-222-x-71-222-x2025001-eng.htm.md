## Summary

A Statistics Canada 'Labour Statistics at a Glance' article (catalogue 71-222-X, released 14 May 2025) reports labour market indicators for persons with and without disabilities in Canada in 2024, based on the Labour Force Survey supplements. As the labour market eased, the employment rate of persons with disabilities fell to 46.4% with sharp declines among youth, their unemployment rate rose less than that of persons without disabilities, and the hourly wage gap widened; the article also examines unionization, benefits, part-time work and persons with disabilities outside the labour force.
