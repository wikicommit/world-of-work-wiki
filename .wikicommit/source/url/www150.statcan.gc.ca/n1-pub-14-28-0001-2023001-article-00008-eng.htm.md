## Summary

This Statistics Canada article in the Quality of Employment in Canada series, released on 13 June 2023, reports the night work indicator: the share of workers whose usual schedule in their main job is a regular night shift or night hours, which was 1.7% (323,000 workers aged 15 to 69) in April 2022 and has been broadly stable since 2016. Drawing mainly on a supplement to the Labour Force Survey, it shows night work was more common among men, workers with less education, landed immigrants and Filipino and Black workers, and in transportation and warehousing, support services, retail and manufacturing, and that night workers were far less likely than daytime workers to have a flexible schedule or to work from home.
