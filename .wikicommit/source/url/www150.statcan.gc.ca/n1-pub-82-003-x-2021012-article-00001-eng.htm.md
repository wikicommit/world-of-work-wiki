## Summary

This Health Reports article by Statistics Canada researchers tests whether items from the federal Public Service Employee Survey (PSES) can measure the 13 workplace psychosocial factors set out in the National Standard of Canada for Psychological Health and Safety in the Workplace. Using exploratory structural equation modelling on the 2017 and 2019 PSES, it identified a 43-item Public Service Psychosocial Questionnaire that captures 10 of the 13 factors plus diversity and inclusion, with full measurement invariance across the two survey years. Preliminary probit regressions pointed to recognition and reward and to involvement and influence as the strongest predictors of job satisfaction.
