## Summary

Statistics Canada's release of the first results from the Canadian Survey on Working Conditions, a new survey collected from September 2024 to July 2025. It reports that 54.3% of workers were frequently exposed to ergonomic risks and 17.0% did unpaid work in their free time several times a month, and describes how physical risks, emotional demands, long workdays and night work, time pressure, schedule flexibility, support from colleagues and managers, and the feeling of doing useful work vary by occupation, industry, education, sex, age and racialized group.
