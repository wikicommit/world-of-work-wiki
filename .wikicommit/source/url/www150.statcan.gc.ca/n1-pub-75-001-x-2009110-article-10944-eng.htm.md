## Summary

A 2009 Perspectives on Labour and Income article using the 2005 General Social Survey to examine work-life balance among Canadian workers aged 55 and over. It finds that 14% of older workers were dissatisfied with their work-life balance (25% of core-age workers), most often because of too much time on the job, and that after correcting for selection out of employment, dissatisfaction was associated with being a woman, having a disability, providing four or more hours of elder care a week, managerial jobs and long hours, while an employed partner, self-employment and job enjoyment lowered it.
