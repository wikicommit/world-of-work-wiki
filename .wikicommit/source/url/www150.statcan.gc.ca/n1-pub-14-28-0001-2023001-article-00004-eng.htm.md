## Summary

A Statistics Canada Quality of Employment in Canada indicator article on the distribution of usual weekly working hours across standardized hour bands, using Labour Force Survey data from 1976 to 2022. Working 40 to less than 45 hours remained the most common schedule (41.0% in 2022, down from 48.1% in 1976), while part-time work rose before 1997 and shares working 35 to less than 40 hours increased; the article also shows how hour bands differ by age, sex and self-employment.
