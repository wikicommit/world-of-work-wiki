## Summary

A Statistics Canada Quality of Employment in Canada indicator article on long working hours — usually working 49 or more hours a week across main and second jobs — using Labour Force Survey data from 1976 to 2023. In 2023, 8.2% of workers in Canada worked long hours, a share that has trended down since 1997, driven mainly by a decline among men, so that the gap between men (11.3%) and women (4.8%) has narrowed. The article also shows higher rates among older workers, some racialized groups, the self-employed, workers in Newfoundland and Labrador, and workers in agriculture and natural resources.
