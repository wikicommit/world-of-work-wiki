## Summary

Statistics Canada's Quality of Employment in Canada indicator article "Evening work, 2022" (released June 13, 2023) reports that in April 2022, 4.8% of workers aged 15 to 69 (918,000 people) usually worked a regular evening shift or evening hours, compared with 6.5% in March 2020 and 5.4% in fall 2016 in earlier surveys. Evening work was concentrated in accommodation and food services (21.8%), among youth aged 15 to 24 (14.2%), especially students, and among part-time workers (11.3% versus 3.3% for full-time). Evening workers had less access to flexible start and end times and found it harder to take time off for personal reasons than workers with regular daytime hours, and almost all worked away from home.
