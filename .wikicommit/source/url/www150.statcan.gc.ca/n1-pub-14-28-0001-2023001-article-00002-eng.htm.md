## Summary

Statistics Canada's Quality of Employment in Canada indicator article "Involuntary part-time work, 1997 to 2022" (released June 13, 2023) uses Labour Force Survey annual averages to show that 556,000 people, or 2.8% of workers, worked part-time involuntarily in 2022 — because of business conditions or because they could not find work of 30 or more hours a week — the lowest rate since the series began in 1997. The rate moves with the business cycle, rising after the 2008/2009 recession and in 2020, and in 2022 was highest among young women and young men, while core-aged men were least likely to be involuntary part-timers but, when they worked part-time, most often did so involuntarily (32.2%). Self-employed workers had a rate twice that of employees.
