## Summary

A Statistics Canada *Insights on Canadian Society* article (December 2023) that profiles self-employment among women in Canada, using Labour Force Survey data from 1976 to 2022 for long-run trends and the 2001 and 2021 Censuses of Population for population groups. It finds that women's share of the self-employed rose from 26% to 37% while their self-employment rate stayed below men's (11% versus 16% in 2022), that self-employed women moved away from unpaid family work in agriculture toward health care and professional services, and that they remain less likely than men to have employees or to be incorporated. Rates and occupations differ markedly across racialized groups, between immigrants and non-immigrants, and between Indigenous and non-Indigenous women.
