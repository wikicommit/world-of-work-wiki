## Summary

A Statistics Canada report in the 'Reports on Disability and Accessibility in Canada' series (catalogue 89-654-X, released 4 June 2026) uses the 2022 Canadian Survey on Disability to examine two forms of labour market underutilization among persons with disabilities aged 25 to 64: work potential among those not working and overqualification among those employed. It finds that 42.0% of non-working persons with disabilities had work potential and about one in three employed persons with disabilities were overqualified, with both measures varying by sociodemographic, disability-related and employment-related characteristics.
