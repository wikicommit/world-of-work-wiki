## Summary

The Australian Bureau of Statistics' Working arrangements release for August 2024 (released 9 December 2024), based mainly on the Characteristics of Employment survey. It reports on casual employment (22% of employees without paid leave entitlements, 20% considering their job casual), fixed-term contracts (4.2% of employees), independent contractors (1.1 million, 7.5% of employed people), working time arrangements such as flexible hours and overtime, working from home (36% of employed people usually worked from home) and digital platform work.
