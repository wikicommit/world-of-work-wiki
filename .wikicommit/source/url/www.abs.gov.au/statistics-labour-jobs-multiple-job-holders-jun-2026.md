## Summary

The Australian Bureau of Statistics' quarterly release on multiple job-holders for June 2026 reports that, in seasonally adjusted terms, there were 1.0 million multiple job-holders, 6.9% of employed people and a record high, with higher rates among women (7.7% against 6.2% for men) and among workers aged 20-24 (11.2%). Drawing on the Labour Account, the Labour Force Survey and the Jobs in Australia (LEED) data, it describes how multiple job-holding varies by industry, occupation, hours worked and geography, and notes that Labour Force Survey-based items are paused during the survey's modernisation.
