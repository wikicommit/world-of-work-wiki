## Summary

The Work and Family Balance section of the Australian Bureau of Statistics release Gender Indicators, Australia, Sep 2018 presents gender indicators on parental leave in the non-public sector, overall life satisfaction, volunteering, providing care, time stress and work–family balance, and time spent on paid and unpaid work. Among its insights, women took 95% of the primary parental leave used by non-public sector employees in 2016–17 while men took 95% of secondary parental leave, and in 2006 women spent nearly twice as long as men on unpaid work while men spent nearly twice as long on employment-related activities.
