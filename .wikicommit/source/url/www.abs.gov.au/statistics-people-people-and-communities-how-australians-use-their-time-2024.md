## Summary

The Australian Bureau of Statistics' 2024 Time Use Survey release describes how Australians aged 15 and over divided their day between personal care, employment and education, unpaid work, and free time. It reports that females spent about an hour more per day on unpaid work than males, that people who worked from home did less paid work but more unpaid work and free time on their workdays, and that a third of people always or often felt rushed or pressed for time.
