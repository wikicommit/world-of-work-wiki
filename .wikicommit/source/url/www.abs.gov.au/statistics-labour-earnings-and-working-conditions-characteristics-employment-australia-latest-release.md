## Summary

The Australian Bureau of Statistics' Characteristics of Employment, Australia release for August 2025 (released 12 December 2025), reporting 2.4 million casual employees, 1.1 million independent contractors, 36% of employed people usually working from home and median weekly employee earnings of $1,425. It also announces that August 2025 was the last time the Characteristics of Employment survey was conducted in its current form, as the ABS replaces the Labour Force Survey's supplementary surveys with a redesigned monthly labour supplementary survey from 2026.
