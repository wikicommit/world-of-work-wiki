## Summary

The Australian Bureau of Statistics' Work-related injuries release for the 2021-22 financial year (released 15 February 2023). It reports that 497,300 people (3.5% of those who worked at some time in the previous 12 months) experienced a work-related injury or illness, down from 4.2% in 2017-18 and 6.4% in 2005-06, and breaks the figures down by sex, age, type and cause of injury, occupation, industry, time off work, workers' compensation and reporting in the workplace.
