## Summary

Insee Première no. 2096 (March 2026) gives Insee's annual snapshot of the French labour market in 2025 from the Labour Force Survey: the employment rate of 15-64-year-olds rose by 0.3 point to 69.3% and the activity rate by 0.6 point to 75.1%, both their highest since 1975, driven by senior employment. The share of fixed-term and temporary agency jobs fell to 9.4%, 19.7% of employees teleworked at least one day a week, part-time work rose to 17.7% and underemployment was almost stable at 4.4%. The ILO unemployment rate rose by 0.3 point to 7.7%, the largest rise since 2013, while the halo around unemployment held at 4.5%.
