## Summary

An Insee Références fact sheet, published on 26 June 2025, on how working time was organised in France in 2024, based on the Insee Labour Force Survey (enquête Emploi). It reports that 46% of employees worked atypical hours (Saturday, Sunday, evening or night) at least once over four weeks, with differences by occupational category and between full-time and part-time work, and that 18% of employees teleworked at least one day a week, mostly managers and professionals in information and communication and financial services.
