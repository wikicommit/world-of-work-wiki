## Summary

The "Durée et conditions de travail" (working time and working conditions) fact sheet of the 2024 edition of Insee's France, portrait social, published on 21 November 2024. It reports usual weekly working hours in France in 2023 (37.0 hours on average, 40.0 full time and 22.7 part time), the shares of people working at atypical times over four weeks, and the accidents at work and occupational diseases recognised in 2019 by the general and agricultural social security schemes.
