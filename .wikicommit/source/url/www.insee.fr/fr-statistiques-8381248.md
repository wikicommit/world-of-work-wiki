## Summary

An Insee Focus study (no. 349, published 4 March 2025) on the gap between women's and men's pay in France's private sector in 2023. Women's average annual salary income was 22.2% below men's, partly because women work fewer hours over the year, but even at equal working time their average full-time-equivalent net salary was 14.2% lower; the salary income gap has narrowed by a third since 1995, faster since 2019, and the study links the remaining gap to age, occupational segregation, under-representation of women among the best-paid jobs and, above all, parenthood, with gaps widening with the number of children.
