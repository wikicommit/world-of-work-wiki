## Summary

A fact sheet on working time and working conditions in France from Insee's France, portrait social 2025, published on 18 November 2025. It reports average usual weekly hours of 36.9 in 2024, the shares of people working Saturdays, Sundays, evenings, nights and at home, and the 679,200 accidents at work with time off and 55,100 occupational diseases recognised in 2022, with their breakdown by sex, age and sector.
