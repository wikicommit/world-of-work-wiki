## Summary

This Insee Première (No 1795, March 2020) uses the 2018 French Labour Force Survey and its complementary module on reconciling work and family life to show that women aged 25–49 with family responsibilities are less often in employment than those without — most markedly among blue-collar women — while the reverse holds for men. Employed women report about twice as often as men that being a parent affects their working life, mainly through reduced or rearranged working time and especially among managers, whereas men and women report similar levels of difficulty reconciling work and family, with managers most affected because of long working days.
