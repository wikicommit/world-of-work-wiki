## Summary

An Insee partenaires publication (No 15, July 2011) produced by Insee with the DJSCS and DRDFEH on reconciling family life and working life. It reports that after a working day nearly half of people say they frequently struggle to do domestic tasks, with lone mothers the most affected and dual-earner couples with children also often in difficulty, while few working people consider that their family life affects their ability to work well, and employers often show flexibility in handling unforeseen events.
