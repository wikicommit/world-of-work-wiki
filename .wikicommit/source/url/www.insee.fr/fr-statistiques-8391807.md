## Summary

An Insee Première study (no. 2044, March 2025) giving an annual picture of the French labour market in 2024 from the Labour Force Survey. The employment rate of 15-64-year-olds reached 68.8%, its highest since 1975, with youth employment falling back and senior employment still rising; fixed-term and temporary-agency jobs continued to decline, 18.2% of employees teleworked at least one day a week, part-time work rose to 17.6%, underemployment stabilised at 4.3%, unemployment stayed at 7.4% and the halo around unemployment fell to 4.5%.
