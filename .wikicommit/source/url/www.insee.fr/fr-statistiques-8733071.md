## Summary

This sheet from Insee's "Emploi, chômage, revenus du travail" (Insee Références, published 2 July 2026) reports that in 2025 full-time employees in France usually worked 38.9 hours a week and actually worked 1,656 hours over the year, slightly below the 2019 level after a sharp fall in 2020 and a recovery to 2023. Managers worked the longest hours, annual hours were higher in market services than in non-market services and lowest in education, women working full-time worked fewer hours than men — with women aged 25–49 in couples with children working less than those living alone, the reverse of men — and the self-employed worked 247 hours more a year than full-time employees.
