## Summary

This dossier in INSEE's Insee Références collection, published on 7 November 2019 and written by researchers from Dares, uses the 2017 Sumer and Reponse surveys to examine how telework affects the working conditions of managers (cadres) in private non-agricultural establishments with more than 10 employees in France. Regular teleworkers benefit from a more flexible framework and less commuting but work longer and more atypical hours without better work–life balance, receive less help from colleagues and superiors, more often face major organisational change and economic insecurity, and report poorer health, while an agreement governing telework appears to play a protective role; overall, teleworkers are neither more nor less satisfied with their work than other managers.
