## Summary

This thematic sheet from Insee's "Emploi et revenus des indépendants" (2025 edition, published 21 May 2025) reports that in 2023 the self-employed in France worked on average 1,971 hours a year in their main job, 422 hours (27%) more than employees, mostly because they worked more days and also longer days, with usual weekly hours of 42.5 against 36.2. Annual hours were highest in food trades, agriculture and accommodation and catering, and fell by 18% between 2008 and 2023, about half of the fall reflecting the growing share of micro-entrepreneurs, who work fewer hours than other self-employed people; the 2020 health crisis cut the self-employed's hours more sharply than employees' and they had not recovered by 2023.
