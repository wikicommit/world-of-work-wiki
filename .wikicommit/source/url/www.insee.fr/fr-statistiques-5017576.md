## Summary

An Insee Première study (no. 1836, January 2021) showing that employees in French retail trade work atypical hours — Saturdays, Sundays, evenings, nights and hours that vary from week to week — more often than other employees of the market services sector, and more often work part time. Based on the Labour Force Surveys of 2017 to 2019, it finds that cashiers and bakers-pastry cooks are the most affected, that younger employees have less standard hours even within the same occupation, that night work is almost exclusively male while women's hours are more irregular, and that 46% of part-time retail employees say they are part time because they cannot work more.
