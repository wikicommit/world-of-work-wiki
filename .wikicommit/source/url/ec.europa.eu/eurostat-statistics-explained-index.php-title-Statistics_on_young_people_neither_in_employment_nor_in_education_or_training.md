## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article presents EU labour force survey statistics on young people neither in employment nor in education or training (NEETs), focusing on 15-29-year-olds, for whom the EU has set a target of 9 % by 2030. In 2025 the EU NEET rate for this group was 11.0 %, ranging from about 5 % in the Netherlands to 19 % in Romania, and it was higher for young women (12.0 %) than young men (9.9 %), for people with a low level of education and for people living in rural areas. The article also defines the indicator and discusses the education-to-work transition and the factors associated with becoming NEET.
