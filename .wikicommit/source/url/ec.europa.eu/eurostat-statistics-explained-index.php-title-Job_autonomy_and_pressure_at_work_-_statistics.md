## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article presents results of the 2019 EU Labour Force Survey ad hoc module on work organisation and working time arrangements: how employees' working hours or presence are recorded, how often employed people work under time pressure, and how much influence they have on the content and order of their tasks. In 2019, 41.1% of EU employees had their working hours recorded automatically or by a supervisor or colleague, 11.0% of employed people always and 25.2% often worked under time pressure, and 49.6% had some or large influence on the content and order of their tasks, with large differences between countries, occupations and professional status.
