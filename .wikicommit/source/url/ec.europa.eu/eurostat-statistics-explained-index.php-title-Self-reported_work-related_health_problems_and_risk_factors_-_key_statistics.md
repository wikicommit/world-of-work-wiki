## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article presents results from the 2007, 2013 and 2020 ad hoc modules of the EU labour force survey on self-reported work-related health problems and exposure to risk factors among people aged 15-64. In 2020, 10.3 % of people who had worked in the EU reported a work-related health problem in the previous 12 months, most often musculoskeletal disorders (6.0 %), followed by stress, depression or anxiety (1.9 %). It also reports that 62.3 % of employed people were exposed to risk factors for their physical health and 44.6 % to risk factors for their mental well-being, with breakdowns by country, sex, age, sector and occupation.
