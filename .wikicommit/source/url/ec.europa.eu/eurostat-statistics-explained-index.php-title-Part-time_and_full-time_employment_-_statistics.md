## Summary

A Eurostat Statistics Explained article presenting EU Labour Force Survey statistics on part-time and full-time employment, with data for 2024. It reports that 17.1% of employed people in the EU worked part-time in 2024, that women (27.8%) were far more likely than men (7.7%) to do so, that part-time work was most common among young people in education, people with low education, women with children and workers on temporary contracts, and it describes how part-time status is self-assessed in the survey and the reasons people give for working part-time.
