## Summary

Eurostat's Statistics Explained article on temporary and permanent employment uses EU Labour Force Survey data to describe trends in temporary employment and the profile of temporary workers by sex, age, education, occupation, presence of children and country. In 2024 temporary employment accounted for 11.0% of employment of people aged 15 to 64 in the EU, the lowest level since 2015, and was far more common among employees aged 15 to 29 (31.1%) than among older age groups; temporary agency workers made up 2.3% of total employment.
