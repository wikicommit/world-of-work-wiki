## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article uses the 2021 EU Labour Force Survey module on the labour market situation of migrants and their immediate descendants to show what obstacles foreign-born people aged 15-74 report in getting a job that matches their education in their EU host country: 23.5% reported obstacles (24.7% of women, 22.2% of men), rising to 26.7% of those who had worked or sought work, and lack of host-country language skills was the most common specific obstacle in 13 of 26 countries. It also breaks the results down by reason for migrating, labour status, education and region of birth, and reports foreign-born people's host-language skills before migrating and their participation in language courses afterwards.
