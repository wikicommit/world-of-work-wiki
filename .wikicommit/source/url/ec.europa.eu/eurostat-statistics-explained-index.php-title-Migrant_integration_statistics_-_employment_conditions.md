## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article compares three employment conditions in the EU for people aged 20-64 by citizenship (nationals, citizens of other EU countries, non-EU citizens) and by migration status, using EU Labour Force Survey data for 2005-2025: the share of self-employment, of employees on temporary contracts and of part-time employment. Non-nationals consistently had less self-employment and more temporary and part-time work than nationals; in 2025, 22.1% of non-EU citizen employees held temporary contracts against 10.7% of nationals.
