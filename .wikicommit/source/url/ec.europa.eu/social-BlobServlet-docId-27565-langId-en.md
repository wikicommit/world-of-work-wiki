## Summary

A European Commission consultation document of 30 April 2024 (C(2024) 2990 final) launching the first-phase consultation of EU social partners under Article 154 TFEU on possible EU action on telework and workers' right to disconnect. It reviews the prevalence of telework and teleworkability, the opportunities and challenges of telework and the 'always-on' culture, the existing EU legal framework and national rules on telework and the right to disconnect, and sets out possible areas and instruments for EU action.
