## Summary

Eurostat's Statistics Explained article on minimum wage statistics compares national minimum wages across EU countries, candidate countries and the United States, as published twice a year for 1 January and 1 July. On 1 July 2026, 22 of the 27 EU countries had a national minimum wage, ranging from €620 a month in Bulgaria to €2 771 in Luxembourg (PPS 935 in Estonia to PPS 2 164 in Germany once price levels are taken into account); the article also reports minimum wages as a share of median gross earnings (the Kaitz index, 44% to 69% in 2024) and the share of employees earning less than 105% of the minimum wage in 2022.
