## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article presents results from the 2023 EU Labour Force Survey module on pensions and labour market participation, covering people aged 50-74. It finds that the transition from work to retirement shifted about five years later between 2012 and 2023, that the average age of first old-age pension receipt was 61.3 years, and that only 13.0% of pensioners kept working after their first pension, for reasons ranging from enjoying work to financial necessity; it also describes pension types, statutory pension ages, early and deferred retirement, and a clustering of countries.
