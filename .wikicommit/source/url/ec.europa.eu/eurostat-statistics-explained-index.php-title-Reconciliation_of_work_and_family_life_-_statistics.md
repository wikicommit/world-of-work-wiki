## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article presents results of the 2018 EU Labour Force Survey ad hoc module on reconciliation between work and family life: who in the EU-28 has care responsibilities for children under 15 or incapacitated relatives, how far professional childcare is used and why not, and how care responsibilities affect employment. In 2018 one in three people aged 18-64 had care responsibilities; 27.1% of employed people adapted their work for childcare, 82% of those who reduced their working hours for childcare were women, and a third of employed women but 1.3% of men had interrupted work for childcare.
