## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article compares labour force participation, employment and unemployment rates in the EU for people aged 20-64 by citizenship (nationals, citizens of other EU countries, non-EU citizens) and by migration status, using EU Labour Force Survey data for 2005-2025. Non-EU citizens have lower participation and employment and higher unemployment than the other groups in most countries, although their unemployment rate fell from 20.0% in 2015 to 12.3% in 2025.
