## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article presents the main results of the 2017 EU Labour Force Survey ad hoc module on self-employment, which sought to identify people at the boundary between employment and self-employment. Using an operational definition based on having no employees, one or a dominant client, and a client who decides working hours, it estimates about 1 million economically dependent self-employed in the EU-28 (3.4% of the self-employed), and compares the self-employed with employees on job autonomy, reasons for self-employment, difficulties, wish to change status and job satisfaction.
