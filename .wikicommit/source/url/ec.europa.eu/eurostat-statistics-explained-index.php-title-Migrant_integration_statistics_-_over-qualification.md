## Summary

Eurostat's Statistics Explained article on over-qualification presents EU Labour Force Survey data on employed people with tertiary education working in low- or medium-skilled occupations, broken down by citizenship, country of birth, sex, age and EU country. Between 2015 and 2025 non-EU citizens consistently had the highest over-qualification rates (41.4% in 2025), against 30.2% for citizens of other EU countries and 20.0% for nationals, with women more affected than men and Greece recording the highest rates for non-EU citizens.
