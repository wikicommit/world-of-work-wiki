## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article presents the European statistics on accidents at work (ESAW) for 2024: 2.78 million non-fatal accidents causing at least four calendar days of absence and 3,367 fatal accidents in the EU, with two-thirds of non-fatal accidents involving men and close to a quarter of fatal accidents occurring in construction. It compares countries using simple and standardised incidence rates per 100,000 employed people, breaks accidents down by economic activity and type of injury, and explains the ESAW definitions, legal basis and the under-reporting and methodological changes that limit cross-country comparison.
