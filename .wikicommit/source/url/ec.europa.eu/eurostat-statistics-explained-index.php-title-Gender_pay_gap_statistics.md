## Summary

This Eurostat Statistics Explained article presents the unadjusted gender pay gap — the difference between men's and women's average gross hourly earnings as a percentage of men's — which stood at 11.1% in the EU in 2024, ranging from -0.8% in Luxembourg to 18.8% in Estonia. It breaks the gap down by part-time and full-time work, age, economic activity and public versus private sector, and explains that the indicator is based on the four-yearly Structure of Earnings Survey and is broader than pay discrimination.
