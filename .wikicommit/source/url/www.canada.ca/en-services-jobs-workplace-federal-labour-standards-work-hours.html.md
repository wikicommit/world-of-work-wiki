## Summary

A Government of Canada information page explaining the hours-of-work provisions of Part III of the Canada Labour Code for federally regulated workplaces. It sets out standard hours of 8 hours a day and 40 hours a week, overtime at 1.5 times the regular wage or equivalent time off, a 48-hour weekly maximum with exceptions, averaging and modified work schedules, breaks, rest periods, advance notice of schedules and shift changes, record keeping, and the special rules for the trucking industry under the Motor Vehicle Operators Hours of Work Regulations.
