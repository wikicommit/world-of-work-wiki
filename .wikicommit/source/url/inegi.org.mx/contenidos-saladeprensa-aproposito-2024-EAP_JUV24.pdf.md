## Summary

INEGI press release 481/24 (9 August 2024), issued for International Youth Day, profiles Mexico's 31 million young people aged 15 to 29 using first-quarter 2024 data from the National Survey of Occupation and Employment (ENOE). It reports that 54.1% of young people were economically active, that 81.3% of employed youth worked as subordinate paid workers, and that their average working week was 40.7 hours and average earnings 33.8 pesos per hour worked. It also describes their occupations, sectors, establishment size, earnings bands and access to benefits such as health services and written contracts.
