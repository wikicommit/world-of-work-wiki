## Summary

A 2023 Eurofound research report that uses a foresight (scenario-building) approach to explore how telework and hybrid work in the EU might develop by 2035. It presents four contrasting scenarios, examines their implications for job quality, organisational practices and health and well-being, discusses cross-cutting issues such as fairness, line managers' skills, regulation, cross-border taxation and gender equality, and sets out policy pointers.
