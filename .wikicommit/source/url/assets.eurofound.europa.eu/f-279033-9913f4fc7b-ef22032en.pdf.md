## Summary

A 2022 Eurofound research report mapping how telework is regulated — through statutory legislation and collective bargaining — in the 27 EU Member States and Norway, and how those regulations changed during the COVID-19 pandemic. It groups countries into six regulatory clusters and compares national provisions on the definition of telework, the telework regime (access, the right to request and the right to return), working time and the right to disconnect, occupational safety and health, and compensation for telework-related expenses, alongside samples of sectoral and company-level collective agreements. It closes with social partners' views on the regulations and policy pointers, including whether the 2002 EU Framework Agreement on Telework still meets current needs.
