## Summary

A 2022 Eurofound report comparing how overtime is defined, limited, compensated and enforced in the EU Member States, Norway and the UK, and assessing its prevalence and extent from national data. It examines the drivers and consequences of overtime, including unpaid overtime and its health effects, and summarises national debates on unpaid working hours, structural overtime, the recording of working time, vulnerable groups and overtime during remote working and the COVID-19 pandemic.
