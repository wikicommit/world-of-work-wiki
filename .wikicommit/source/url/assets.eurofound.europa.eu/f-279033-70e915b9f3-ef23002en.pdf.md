## Summary

A 2023 Eurofound report on the right to disconnect that reviews national legislation and collective agreements in EU Member States and presents a December 2022 survey of employees in Belgium, France, Italy and Spain working in companies with and without a right to disconnect policy. It finds that such policies do not reduce out-of-hours contact or responses to it, but are associated with fewer additional hours worked because of out-of-hours contact, more compensation for additional hours, better work–life balance, fewer reported health problems and higher satisfaction with working conditions.
