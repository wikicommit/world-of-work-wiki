## Summary

A 2023 Eurofound report reviewing developments in working time regulation and collective bargaining in the EU and Norway in 2021–2022, notably the transposition of the Work–life Balance Directive and the Transparent and Predictable Working Conditions Directive, new rules on recording working time and telework, and debates on the four-day week. It reports statutory maximum weekly hours, collectively agreed weekly hours (an EU27 average of 38.1 in 2022), usual weekly hours, paid annual leave (an EU27 average of 24.3 days) and estimated collectively agreed annual working time (1,726 hours in the EU27).
