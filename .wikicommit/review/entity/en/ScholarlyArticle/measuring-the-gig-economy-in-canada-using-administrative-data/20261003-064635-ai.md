The occupation bullet in Key Points opens with 'By main occupation' but then says male and female gig workers were concentrated in particular industries (professional, scientific and technical services; construction; health care and social assistance). The source gives those as industry-of-gig-work distributions (Table 3), not main-occupation ones. Its occupation distributions (Table 4) differ, for example trades and transport for men and sales and service for women. A reader could take the industry list as an occupational finding.
The 24.2% and 26.6% art, culture, recreation and sport figures are stated in the source text as prevalence among workers in that occupation, which is how the page reads them. The Table 4 data table files the same numbers under 'Share of all workers'. The page follows the source prose.
