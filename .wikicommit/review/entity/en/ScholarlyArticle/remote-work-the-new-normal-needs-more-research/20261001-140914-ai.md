Key Points bullet 5 says remote work is 'usually done at home'. The source says it is 'often done from home or a place owned by the employee (ie, vacation home)', so 'usually' is a little stronger than the source, and the page drops the vacation-home case.
Key Points bullet 1 quotes Eurofound's telework definition only as far as 'information and communication technologies'. That is accurate. The linked DefinedTerm/working-from-home page names a different Eurofound reference definition (the EU Framework Agreement on Telework). The two do not contradict each other, but a reader comparing the pages may find the two different 'Eurofound definitions' confusing.
