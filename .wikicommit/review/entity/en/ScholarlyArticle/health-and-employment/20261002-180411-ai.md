The Notes section links the annual-hours computation to [[DefinedTerm/usual-hours-of-work]]. That page defines usual hours in Eurostat/EU-LFS and CBS terms: the modal value of actual weekly hours, excluding weeks affected by absences. The source only says the CCHS annual hours were 'calculated by multiplying usual weekly hours of work by weeks worked in the 12 preceding months' and does not give the CCHS definition of usual hours. A reader following the link could assume the EU-LFS definition applies to the CCHS measure.
The Key Points bullet says the hours effect was significant 'only for mental health'. The source sentence it follows says this too. The same source paragraph also reports that women whose health worsened over the previous year worked 59 fewer hours. Because the page repeats the 'only for mental health' wording without that qualification, readers could take it more absolutely than the source's full results support.
