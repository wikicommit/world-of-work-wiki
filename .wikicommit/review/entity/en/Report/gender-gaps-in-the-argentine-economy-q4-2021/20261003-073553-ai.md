Those INDEC documents are not in the assembled evidence; the figures come only from this report's own text of them. The page links [[DefinedTerm/gender-pay-gap]] from the income paragraph, but that term is defined as a gap in average gross hourly earnings among employees. The report measures gaps in monthly income (total individual income, main-occupation income, informal wage earners), so a reader could take the link as implying the same indicator.
The source gives the domestic-service share of women's employment inconsistently (98.4% female and 12.2% of employed women in one place, 98.2% and 12.4% in another). The page uses the first set, which matches the report's table and its main text.
