The page follows the study's own definition of atypical hours, which includes hours that vary or alternate from week to week and scores five kinds of hours. The linked DefinedTerm/atypical-working-hours page says Insee reports varying hours separately from the atypical-hours measure and counts only four kinds of working time. This is not a contradiction, because the source says its definition applies 'dans cette étude'. A reader following the link may still see the two as inconsistent.
The page says part-time retail employees who cannot work more have varying hours more often 'than other employees (45% against 30%)'. The source's comparison group is all retail employees ('l'ensemble des salariés'), so 'other' is slightly imprecise. The same applies to the 21% versus 10% fixed-term contract figures.
