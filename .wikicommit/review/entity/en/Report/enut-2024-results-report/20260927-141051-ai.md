The 'Satisfaction with time use' paragraph opens with 'Among people aged 20 to 49 who did economic activity in the reference week' and then gives the domestic-work (15.2% / 6.4%) and care (68.2% / 62.4%) figures. Read in sequence, those figures seem to apply to the same economically active group. The source (lines 1548-1549, 1571-1572, 1585-1586) bases each of them on everyone aged 20 to 49 who did that particular activity. Only the first figure is limited to people who did economic activity.
The body links [[DefinedTerm/unpaid-work]] for 'the rest [of total work time] was unpaid work'. The existing page defines that term using the ABS Time Use Survey scope: domestic activities, childcare, adult care and volunteering. That scope leaves out production of goods for the household's own consumption, which ENUT counts as unpaid work (source lines 122-127, 194-197). No single fact conflicts between the two pages, but a reader following the link gets a narrower definition than the one this report uses.
