The page links [[DefinedTerm/atypical-working-hours]], whose definition is the Labour Force Survey four-week measure (Saturday, Sunday, evening or night work at least once). This report uses a different definition: usual hours that are long, shifted or split, plus at least 11 Saturdays or Sundays worked a year. The two use different thresholds and both appear in the wiki, so a reader following the link could mistake them for the same measure, and the 48% figure here is not comparable to the term page's 46% for 2024.
