Replies and revision paragraph: 'most of the provisions on personal data ... went to the Recommendation or were removed' is easy to misread. The brown report (V(3), paras 86-87) proposed moving only subparagraphs (a)-(d) of Article 19, and V(4) did move them, to new Recommendation Paragraph 16. It kept Article 18 and the first part of Article 19's chapeau in the Convention as a single principles-based Article (new Article 15; V(4) lines 3064-3069, 6502-6506). A reader could come away thinking most personal-data protection left the Convention. 'The detailed prohibitions on processing certain personal data' would be more precise.
Draft Convention paragraph: the Article 17 sentence runs the two limbs together. As written, both the written explanation and the human review apply only to decisions refusing payment, suspending or deactivating an account, or ending engagement. In the source (V(3) lines 2185-2193), Article 17(a) gives a written explanation for any decision that affects working conditions or access to work. Only 17(b), the human review, is limited to that narrower list of decisions.
