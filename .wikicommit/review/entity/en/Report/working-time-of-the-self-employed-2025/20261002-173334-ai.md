The page links the Insee definition of the self-employed (no legal subordination to a client, no employment contract; non-salaried workers plus salaried company directors) to [[DefinedTerm/self-employment]]. That page is built on the Statistics Canada definition, which also counts unpaid family workers and distinguishes incorporated from unincorporated. A reader following the link may take the two definitions to be the same.
The sentence that more than two-thirds of the gap comes from more days worked, with the rest from longer days, combines the source's 'plus des deux tiers' with its separate remark that daily hours are also longer. The source gives no exact split, so the 'rest' phrasing is an inference from that remark.
Figure 2's field is metropolitan France only for 2003–2013 and France excluding Mayotte from 2014, whereas the page states the France-excluding-Mayotte scope without that qualification. This matters for the 2008 to 2023 comparison.
