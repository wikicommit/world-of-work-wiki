The intro renders the source's sub-topic 'Time Stress and Work and Family Balance' as 'time stress and [[DefinedTerm/work-life-balance]]'. The source measures work–family balance, which the linked term page describes as one component of work–life balance rather than the same thing. A reader may take the ABS indicator to be a broader work–life measure than it is.
The page says women spent 'about half as long' as men on employment-related activities, while the source says 'nearly half'. The figures (2h21m against 4h33m) support either wording, but the page drops the source's 'nearly'.
The page says balancing work and family was 'the main reason' for both sexes. The source lists two main reasons for each sex, with balance the larger of the two. For men the gap is narrow: 27% against 26% for pressure of work or study.
