The Costs paragraph says the equivalent annual net direct cost to business is £100–450 million a year, then lists the £170–420 million workforce-planning cost among the monetised costs. The source (paras 257, 263) says workforce planning is treated as an indirect cost and is not part of that direct-cost figure, but it is part of the net present social value. A reader could take the £100–450 million figure as including workforce planning.
The Enforcement paragraph says the Fair Work Agency reaches only payment for 'cancelled or curtailed' shifts. The source wording is 'cancelled, moved or curtailed'. This is a minor shortening and does not change the meaning.
