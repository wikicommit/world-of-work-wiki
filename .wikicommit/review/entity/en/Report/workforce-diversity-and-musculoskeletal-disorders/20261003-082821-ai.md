The Health and MSDs paragraph says LGBTI workers report "more musculoskeletal problems than the general population" and attributes this to the fieldwork. The report's own section 3.4 says there is no clear or conclusive evidence of increased MSD risk among LGBTI workers and that MSD prevalence for this group is expected rather than measured. The summary and conclusions do state worse physical health "including musculoskeletal problems", so the sentence is supported. A reader could still take it as firmer than the report's caveats allow.
