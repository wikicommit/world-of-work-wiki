The 2019–2022 sentence says men's full-time hours 'accounted for most of the 0.3-hour fall'. The source calls this the 'largest contributor' at negative 0.5 hours, which is larger than the total fall it is described as 'most of'. Offsetting positive effects (the +0.2 share effect and part-time increases) explain the gap. 'Most of' is not wrong in spirit, but a reader could misread it as a component smaller than the total.
