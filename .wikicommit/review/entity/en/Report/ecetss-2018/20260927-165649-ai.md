The intro describes DGEMyEL as 'the Ministry of Production and Labour's directorate for macroeconomic studies and labour statistics.' The source actually places DGEMyEL under the Secretaría de Gobierno de Trabajo y Empleo, which is itself dependent on the Ministry of Production and Labour (MPyT) — the page's phrasing skips that intermediate Secretariat layer. This reads as a defensible compression rather than an invented fact, since DGEMyEL does ultimately sit within the Ministry's structure, but a reader could come away thinking DGEMyEL reports directly to the Ministry.
