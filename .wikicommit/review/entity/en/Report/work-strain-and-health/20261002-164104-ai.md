In the 'Most common strains' paragraph, the figures '(20.1% and 21.5%)' follow the list 'Heavy lifting and carrying, awkward postures and shift work', so they read as applying to all three. In the source they belong to shift work alone. Lifting and carrying is 23.3% of women and 30.7% of men, and awkward postures is 23.6% and 28.0%. The page does not misstate any individual figure, but a reader could take the shared parenthetical at face value.
