The page links the fact sheet's "sandwiched" parents to [[DefinedTerm/sandwich-generation]]. That page defines the term as people caring for elderly relatives and young children. The fact sheet's group is broader: parents of children aged 17 and under who care for a family member or friend with a chronic health condition, a disability or problems related to aging (source lines 129–134). A reader following the link could take the two groups to be the same.
