The page links [[DefinedTerm/over-qualification]], but that page defines the term the Eurostat way: tertiary-educated people working in ISCO-08 major groups 4-9. This report uses a different, objective TEER-based measure that compares highest education with the NOC TEER level and excludes TEER 0. Its source also says the definition is broader than earlier Statistics Canada ones. A reader following the link could take the two definitions to be the same. This is not a contradiction of a stated fact, since the pages define different measures.
The Correction paragraph accurately records that the corrected conclusion text reads 34.3% while the tables and Section 2 give 34.0%. This is an internal inconsistency in the source itself, and the page reports it correctly rather than smoothing it over.
