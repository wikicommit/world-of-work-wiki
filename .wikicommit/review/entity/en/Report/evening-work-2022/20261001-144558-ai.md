'Statistics Canada attributes the lower 2022 rate partly to employment in accommodation and food services' drops the source's hedge: the article says the lower rate 'may be in part due to the decline in employment in the accommodation and food services industry'. The companion DefinedTerm/evening-work page keeps the hedge ('may partly reflect').
'The article draws the same contrast with a companion indicator on workers who usually work at night' is ambiguous: the source says evening workers, 'in a similar fashion to workers who usually work at night', mostly worked exclusively away from home (94.1% vs 72.2% of daytime workers). It is a similarity with night workers, not a contrast between evening and night workers; the sentence can be misread as the latter.
