The four-types figures in the lead (10h43m, 8h20m, 4h25m, 5h34m) are averages for the people who took part in each type, as the source's table footnote says. They add up to more than 24 hours, so they cannot be read as one person's day. The page says 'Among the people who took part in each', which covers this, but the phrase 'the average day held' could still be misread as one combined 24-hour day.
The source ties the working-from-home unpaid-work comparison (3h24m vs 2h26m) to 'people who worked from home on their diary day'. The page puts it under 'On workdays'. The two readings match in substance, but the page's wording is slightly looser than the source's.
