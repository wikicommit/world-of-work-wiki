Details, paragraph 1: 'the COVID-19 pandemic, which, citing Eurofound, it describes as sharply increasing home-based telework and possibly marking the start of a lasting trend towards hybrid work' spreads the Eurofound citation over the whole sentence. In the source (C 143/12 and footnotes 4-5), Eurofound is cited only for the telework prevalence figures. The prediction that the pandemic 'may mark the start of a lasting trend towards more home-based telework ... hybrid work' is the Commission's own statement. The wording could be read as attributing that prediction to Eurofound.
Summary, paragraph 1: 'consolidates into one document all the relevant case-law from before and after 2017 — more than 80 judgments and orders since 1993' joins two separate source statements. The source says the Communication consolidates 'all relevant case-law issued before and after the 2017 Interpretative Communication'. Separately, it says 'Since 1993, more than 80 judgments and orders of the Court have dealt with the Working Time Directive'. The source never says the Communication covers all 80+ rulings.
Details, paragraph 3: the page lists the definitions as 'night time and night work'. The source's Chapter IV.B heading and Article 2(3)-(4) define 'night time' and 'night worker'. 'Night work' is discussed but is not the defined term there.
