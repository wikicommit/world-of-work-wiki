The page says harassed unionized workers were "more likely to say the harassment had been addressed". The source says they were more likely to say they had *tried to address* it, and gives the "had been addressed" wording only for verbal abuse (12% versus 6%). A reader could take this as a broader outcome claim than the source makes.
The well-being paragraph lists plans to leave the job among the associations with harassment without qualification. The source says that for women this association disappears once other factors are adjusted for. The page's "more likely" wording is true of the unadjusted figures and of men's adjusted figures only.
