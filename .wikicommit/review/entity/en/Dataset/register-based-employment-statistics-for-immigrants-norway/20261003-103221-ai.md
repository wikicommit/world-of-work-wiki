The country-background groupings ('EU/EFTA etc.' / 'Africa, Asia etc.') and the full-time definition come from the article's 'Datagrunnlaget' box, which describes how the article itself uses the statistic ('Ved omtale av landbakgrunn brukes fellesbetegnelsen…'). The page presents them as the dataset's own groupings and definitions ('SSB groups countries into…'). This is a reasonable reading, but it widens their scope from the article to the whole statistic.
The page defines full time as main job plus secondary jobs combined. The source also uses a main-job-only position percentage when it breaks results down by occupation (Figure 7). The page does not misstate this, but a reader could assume the full-time share by occupation uses the combined measure.
