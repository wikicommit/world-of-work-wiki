The page links [[DefinedTerm/over-qualification]] as the concept the 2022 CSD report measures. That linked page describes Eurostat's ISCO-08 tertiary-education definition. The source report instead uses an objective measure comparing educational attainment with the occupation's NOC TEER level. A reader following the link could take the two measures as the same. The page makes no definitional claim about it, so this is not a defect here.
The Variables paragraph says employment questions refer to the job held in the reference week or, failing that, the longest-held job since 1 January 2020. In the source this rule is stated for building the qualification-status concept, and only for the majority of cases. The page presents it as applying to the survey's employment questions generally.
