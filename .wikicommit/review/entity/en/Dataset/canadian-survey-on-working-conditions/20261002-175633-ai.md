The body says the survey gives 'a fuller picture of [[DefinedTerm/job-quality]] in Canada', but the source's term is 'quality of employment', and it links to Statistics Canada's quality of employment framework. The linked job-quality page defines the term through Eurofound's seven-dimension framework. A reader could take the link to mean that Statistics Canada uses Eurofound's job quality framework. The source credits Eurofound only for the concepts behind the physical risk factors.
The page uses the CSWC definition of working at night (at least 2 hours between 10:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m.) and links it to [[DefinedTerm/night-work]]. The 'In statistics' section of that page describes a different Statistics Canada measure: a usual schedule of a regular night shift or night hours, from the LFS-based Quality of Employment indicator. The two pages do not contradict each other. Still, a reader following the link could mix up the two Statistics Canada measures.
