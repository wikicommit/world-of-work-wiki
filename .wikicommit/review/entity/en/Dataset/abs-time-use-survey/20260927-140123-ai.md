Details paragraph: 'The ABS has run the survey in 1992, 1997, 2006, 2020-21 and 2024' is a slight overreach. The source (line 95) says only that the 2024 TUS 'was conducted by the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS)' and that 'Previous surveys were conducted in 1992, 1997, 2006 and 2020-21'. It does not name who ran the earlier cycles. A safer wording would be 'The survey was previously conducted in 1992, 1997, 2006 and 2020-21'.

This is a small change of framing, not a factual error.
