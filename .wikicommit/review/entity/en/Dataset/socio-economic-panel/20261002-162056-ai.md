The lede says the SOEP 'now covers more than 30,000 people in about 15,000 households'. The 'now' comes from the 2023 report's 'mittlerweile', but the page does not date it, so it reads as current as of the page's generation. It also sits beside the 2019 discussion paper's 'about 20,000 people in some 11,000 households' further down. Both figures are correctly attributed to their own sources, but without a reference year on the first one, a reader may take them as conflicting rather than as two snapshots.
'About 15,000 households' blends two phrasings in the same report. Section 3 says '15.000 Haushalten' and the summary says 'knapp 15.000 Haushalten' (just under 15,000). This is not wrong, but 'just under' is lost.
