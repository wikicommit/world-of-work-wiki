The sentence 'agency workers may see themselves as visitors rather than members of the work group, with weaker social relations and trust' combines two different studies. In the source, the 'visitor' finding comes from Viitala & Kantola (2016). The trust finding comes from Svensson (2012), and it concerns agency workers' generally lower trust in other people, not trust inside the work group. Read as one sentence, it suggests both findings come from a single study and that the trust is trust within the work group.
The paragraph beginning 'A Swedish survey of just under 500 agency workers' describes one study (Håkansson & Isidorsson 2016) through several clauses, but the source draws two of the 'found' points from more than one study. The finding that agency workers have less access to competence development is attributed to Håkansson & Isidorsson (2018) and Augustsson (2014), and the page gives it in the previous sentence as a general finding of the studies. It is placed correctly, but the page's paraphrase removes the source's caveat that these results do not link explicitly to health.
