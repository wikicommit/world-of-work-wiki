The opening definition uses the EU-OSHA report's gloss of prolonged sitting; where the report uses 'sedentary work' it glosses it as computer work and sitting, so the page's definition is narrower than that usage.
The ESENER 74% figure is an upper bound for employees who sit for long periods, per the report's footnote 149.
The points on short sitting being beneficial and an unestablished threshold come from works the EU-OSHA report cites, not its own analysis.
