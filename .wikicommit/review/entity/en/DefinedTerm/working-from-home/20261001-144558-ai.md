Usage opens with 'Several overlapping terms are used for the same idea, including remote working, teleworking and telecommuting', and the frontmatter aliases list Remote working / Telework / Teleworking / Telecommuting as synonyms. The Productivity Commission source says only that these terms are 'associated with the idea of working from home' (Box 1.1). The page's own later paragraphs, following Eurofound and Service Public, then present telework and remote work as broader concepts that include workplaces other than the home. A reader could take the opening sentence and the aliases as saying these terms are equivalent to working from home, which the rest of the page contradicts. Rewording to 'associated' or 'related' terms would match the source.
In the SJWEH paragraph, 'The authors suggest organizations give employees autonomy over how, where and how much they telework' merges two separate sentences of the abstract. The first finds that more autonomy in telework decisions is associated with reduced physiological stress. The second says the results 'can be used by organizations to improve telework conditions (how, where and how much)'. The authors do not explicitly recommend granting autonomy over all three. This is a fair paraphrase, but it is slightly stronger than the source.
