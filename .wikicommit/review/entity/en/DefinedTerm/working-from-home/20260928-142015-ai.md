Round 1: Luxembourg 'regulates [occasional telework] separately' was a slight paraphrase stretch (corrected in round 2). 'Eurofound treats telework as equivalent to TICTM' was scoped in the source to that report (corrected in round 2).
The frontmatter lists 'Telework' and 'Teleworking' as aliases, and the Usage section says remote working, teleworking and telecommuting are 'used for the same idea' (PC Box 1.1 only says these terms are 'associated with the idea'). Later paragraphs say telework is broader than working at home, so a reader could take the aliases to mean exact equivalents.
properties.description gives the Productivity Commission's definition without attribution; the body attributes it.
'Telework is set up by a collective agreement or an employer charter' is stronger than Service Public's 'peut être mis en place'; the same sentence gives the employer–employee agreement alternative, so the meaning holds.
'Portugal removed its regularity requirement (habitualidade) in 2021': the removing law is dated 6 December 2021 and took effect on 1 January 2022, so '2021' is the year the law was passed, not the year it took effect.
