The frontmatter `properties.description` gives the fair-income / workplace-security / personal-development / equality-of-opportunity wording as a plain definition. The body correctly presents it as a formulation Eurostat cites from the European Commission (source lines 228, footnote 11), so a reader of the description alone would take one party's formulation as the settled definition.
The body says "Eurostat notes" that solo self-employment can point to entrepreneurial initiative but can also be a proxy for "bogus" self-employment. In the source (line 244, footnote 16) that framing is cited to the European Commission's 2024 Proposal for a Joint Employment Report, so "Eurostat notes" is accurate only in the sense that Eurostat repeats it.
"Fair income and social protection are tracked through in-work poverty" reads as if in-work poverty is one of the goal's main indicators. The source lists six main indicators and does not include it; it treats in-work poverty as an important component of decent work and refers readers to SDG 1.
