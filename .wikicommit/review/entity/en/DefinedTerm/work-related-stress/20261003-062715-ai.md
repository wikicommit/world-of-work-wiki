The paragraph on workers' attributed causes (workload, lack of managerial support, violence and bullying, organisational change, role uncertainty) sits among the 2024/25 and 2022/23-2024/25 figures. The HSE source draws it from a much older LFS period, 2009/10-2011/12, and the page does not give that date, so a reader could take it as current.
"Followed by a lack of managerial support, violence and bullying, organisational change and role uncertainty" reads as a ranking. The source text only says workload was the main cause and lists the others as "other factors identified"; it states no order among them.
The intro says the leaflet treats prevention as improving working conditions "as well as supporting individuals". The leaflet stresses that the approach is aimed at the organisation rather than individuals, and mentions individual concerns only as local systems to respond to them, so the "as well as" phrasing slightly overstates the individual-support side.
The workplace-size sentence ("medium and large workplaces") follows the source, which says both have statistically significantly higher rates. The source's wording on this point is repetitive and may be a typo, so it could be worth rechecking against the HSE table.
