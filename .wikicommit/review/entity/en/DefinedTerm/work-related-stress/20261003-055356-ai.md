The Eurostat figures (1.9 %, Sweden 8.6 %, etc.) cover the combined category 'stress, depression or anxiety'. Respondents also report only their most serious work-related health problem. A reader could take these as prevalence of work-related stress alone, and the page's wording does not flag the combined category or the most-serious-problem limit.
The opening sentence says HSE treats prevention as improving working conditions 'as well as supporting individuals'. The leaflet stresses an organisation-level approach 'rather than individuals', with individual support limited to local systems for individual concerns and the line manager's role. The 'as well as' wording is slightly softer than the leaflet's emphasis.
