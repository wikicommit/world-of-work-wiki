The sentence "Non-EU citizens' unemployment was highest in Sweden, Finland and Spain" is easy to misread. The source says only that high levels were observed in those three countries (22.8%, 20.7%, 16.7%). It does not rank them as the highest in the EU.
In the education paragraph, "the differences across education levels are smaller for foreign-born people and for the native-born with two foreign-born parents" is stated in the source only for labour force participation. The page places it after a clause covering both participation and employment, so it reads as applying to employment too.
"The gap reverses in favour of native-born people with two native-born parents" slightly paraphrases the source, which says the employment gap "widens" among the tertiary-educated. The reversal is implied by the preceding low-education contrast but is not stated in those words.
