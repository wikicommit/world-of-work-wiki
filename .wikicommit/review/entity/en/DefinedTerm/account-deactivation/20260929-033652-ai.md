The 'Why it is an issue' paragraph says the parameters of algorithmic and rating-based decisions are 'often not disclosed'. The source (para. 192) says they are 'not necessarily disclosed in service agreements', which is weaker and hedged. The page's wording states it as more frequent than the source does.
On the cross-border point, the page says a worker no longer entitled to work in a country 'may be unable to stay there to pursue a claim'. The source (para. 195) only says that this prospect 'also requires consideration'. The page states a consequence where the source raises an open issue.
The `description` says deactivation is 'often decided with the help of algorithms and customer ratings'. The source supports this only as studies documenting automatic deactivation based on low customer ratings, plus algorithms and ratings leading to disconnection. 'Often' is a generalisation the report does not quantify.
