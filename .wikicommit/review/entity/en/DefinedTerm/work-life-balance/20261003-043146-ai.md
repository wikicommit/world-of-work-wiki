The Usage section pairs 'the flexibility to combine paid work with other tasks' with commute savings as ways working from home can improve balance. The Commission's chapter 5 passage names only the time gained by avoiding the commute as the balance benefit. The flexibility link comes from its chapters 2 and 5 on flexibility and wellbeing, so a reader could take the pairing as stated in one place.
The HILDA satisfaction measure is specifically satisfaction with the flexibility to balance work and non-work commitments, not a general work–life balance score. The page words it correctly, but the figure caption calls it 'work–life balance satisfaction'.
