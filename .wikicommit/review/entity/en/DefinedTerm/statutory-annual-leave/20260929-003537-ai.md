The *Entitlement* bullet says flatly that no leave accrues 'during parental leave'. Source 3 does say this in section VII.A.1 (lines 2750-2751). But its fuller treatment in VII.C.2 (lines 3499-3503) narrows it: the period doesn't count as actual work only where national law suspends the employment relationship. It also says leave accrued before parental leave cannot lapse during it (lines 3495-3497). The page's unqualified wording is broader than that fuller treatment.
The sentence 'Member States may regulate how the right is exercised — for example the planning of holidays or advance notice' draws on the quoted list at Source 3, lines 2889-2893. That list comes from the Commission's own submission in BECTU (footnote 238), not from a Court holding. Under the page's 'Commission's Interpretative Communication outlines the Court's case-law' framing, a reader could take it as case-law. The general point is also backed by Court case-law at lines 2874-2877, so this is not a defect.
