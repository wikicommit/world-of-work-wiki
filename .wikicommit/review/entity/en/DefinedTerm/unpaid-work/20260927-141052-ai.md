The Usage sentence 'The ENUT reports passive care separately from active care' is supported in substance: the source's care figures carry the note 'excluye cuidados pasivos', and Gráfica 31 adds passive care back in. However, 'active care' is the page's own label. INEGI never uses the term; it only defines 'cuidados pasivos' as a simultaneous or secondary activity. A reader could take 'active care' to be an ENUT category.
The opening sentence generalizes from two national surveys to 'time-use surveys' in general, saying they measure unpaid work 'alongside, and separately from, paid work'. Both sources support this for their own survey (ABS: 'paid and unpaid work'; ENUT: 'mide tanto el trabajo remunerado como el no remunerado'). Neither source makes the claim about time-use surveys in general.
