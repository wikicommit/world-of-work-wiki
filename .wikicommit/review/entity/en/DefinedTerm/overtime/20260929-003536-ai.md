The opening paragraph says 'Eurofound defines overtime work as…'. Source 2 actually introduces that definition as the one in 'Eurofound's European Industrial Relations Dictionary' (lines 99-109). Naming the dictionary would be more precise, though the attribution to Eurofound is not wrong.
The gloss of ordinary working time as 'the hours a worker is normally required to work under the law, a collective agreement or the employment contract' is a generalisation the page builds itself. Source 1 only says 'work performed in excess of ordinary working time'. Source 2 places the threshold at normal working time set 'through collective bargaining or … by legislation', with work 'beyond the parameters established in their employment contracts and in regulations or legislation'. So the gloss is supported in substance, but no single source states it in these words.
