The lead paragraph says supervisory care is 'measured separately from actively looking after children'. The source (line 302) says only that 'Supervisory care of children while doing another activity is measured separately'; 'actively' is the page's own gloss. The source's 'Looking after children' table (line 329) also has a 'Supervising or watching child' category inside looking after children, so a reader could wrongly take the page to mean that all supervising sits outside unpaid work. Tying the contrast to 'while doing another activity', as the source does, would be more precise.
The 2 hours 49 minutes figure for supervising while doing unpaid work is correctly left without 'a day', matching the source (line 368). The other two averages do carry 'a day' in the source.
