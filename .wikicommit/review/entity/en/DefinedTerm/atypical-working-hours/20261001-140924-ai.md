The page links Insee's survey category 'horaires alternés (2x8, 3x8, équipes)' to [[DefinedTerm/shift-work]], a page built on the EU Working Time Directive's legal definition. The two are close but not the same concept. A reader could take the Insee 13%/6% alternating-shift figures to be measuring shift work in the Directive's sense.
The Usage paragraph gives two Saturday figures, 36% (employees, source 1) and 41% (all people in employment, source 2). The page labels the two populations correctly, but the figures sit close together and could be misread as conflicting.
