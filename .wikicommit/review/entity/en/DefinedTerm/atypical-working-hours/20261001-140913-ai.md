The page defines 'night' as midnight to 5 a.m. under Insee's statistical measure. The linked night-work page describes legal night periods: under EU law, at least seven hours including midnight to 5 a.m.; in the UK, normally 11pm to 6am. The two pages do not contradict each other, because the page under review scopes its definition to Insee. Still, a reader following the link could take Insee's narrower 00h-5h window for a legal night period. A short note that the statistical window differs from the legal ones would help.
The page links Insee's 'horaires habituels alternés (2x8, 3x8, équipe, etc.)' to DefinedTerm/shift-work. That page carries the broader EU legal definition. The link is reasonable, but the 6% and 13% figures measure Insee's survey category, not shift work as the Directive defines it.
