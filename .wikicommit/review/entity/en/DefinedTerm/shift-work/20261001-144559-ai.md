Measurement section: the sentence on sleep problems among evening, rotating and irregular shift workers follows 'Using the National Population Health Survey...', but the article draws those sleep figures from the 2000/01 Canadian Community Health Survey. A reader could take them for NPHS findings.
Measurement section: 'Most shift workers changed schedule or left the workforce within a few years'. The article's concluding remarks say 'Within two years'. The page's wording is looser than the source but does not contradict it.
Cross-page (not a defect): this page gives WorkSafe NZ's advice as avoiding shift starts before 7 am, from 'Managing the risks of shift work'. DefinedTerm/work-related-fatigue gives 'avoid starts before 6 am', from WorkSafe's separate Fatigue quick guide. Each page follows its own source, but readers may take them for one recommendation that disagrees with itself.
The frontmatter description ('according to a pattern, which may be rotating and continuous or discontinuous') shortens Article 2(5) ('a certain pattern, including a rotating pattern, and which may be continuous or discontinuous'). It could be read as saying the pattern must be rotating.
