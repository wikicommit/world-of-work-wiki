The page says the concept of contractual hours "does not exist for people who do not work fixed hours". The source (section 3.1) says only that the contractual working time "is not available" ("niet beschikbaar") for people without fixed hours in the survey. "Not collected or available" is the closer wording.
The opening definition (hours "without taking into account extra hours worked and paid or fewer hours worked and not paid") comes from the source's technical note on the Polisadministratie variable. The page presents it as the general definition, although it is not specific to the Labour Force Survey variable.
