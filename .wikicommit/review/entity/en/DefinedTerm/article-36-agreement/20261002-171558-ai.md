The sentence "The pamphlet treats these limits as a reference point for preventing overwork" is looser than the pamphlet's wording. The pamphlet says the agreement must conform to the limit standard. Separately, its sample policy sets overtime at no more than 45 hours a month in principle. The 45-hour policy is a pamphlet example, not a statement about the Article 36 limits.
The parenthetical "([[DefinedTerm/karoshi]])" after the physician-interview link is ambiguous. It could be read as saying that the interview guidance is itself karoshi, rather than that it relates to preventing karoshi.
