The page says De Witte, Vander Elst and De Cuyper 'define it as "an overall concern about the continued existence of the job in the future"'. The source's wording is passive: De Witte et al. 'anger att ... anställningsotrygghet ... har definierats som ...' ('state that job insecurity ... has been defined as ...'). That leaves open whether the definition is their own or one they are reporting. The page's direct 'define it as' reads slightly stronger than the source. Wording such as 'describe it as having been defined as' would match the source more closely.
The page calls the Danish subjects 'hotel cleaners'. The source's word 'hotellstäderskor' supports this, but it refers to women hotel cleaners, and the cited study's title calls the work 'Housekeeping in Danish hotels'. The reading is acceptable and is not a defect.
