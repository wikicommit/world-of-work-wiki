The Usage section reports shares on different bases without saying so. The 11.0% figure, the 13.8% peak and the 12.2% / 10.0% sex split are shares of total employment among people aged 15 to 64. The 31.1% / 7.4% / 4.6% age figures and the reason percentages are shares of employees, and the country range (1.4% to 22.2%) is for the working-age population. A reader could easily compare these as if they shared one denominator.

The sentence that Eurostat applies the distinction 'only to employees' is an inference. The source says permanency is collected only for the main job, and it places the self-employed and unpaid family workers in 'employment other than temporary' (source lines 140-142).
