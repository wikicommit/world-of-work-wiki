The frontmatter description calls total work time 'the headline measure' of the ENUT. The source does not say this. It only places TTT first among its principal results (lines 190-220). That is an interpretive gloss, not a stated fact.
The body links [[DefinedTerm/unpaid-work]] while using the ENUT scope of unpaid work. Under ENUT (source lines 122-127), unpaid work includes own-consumption production of goods, community work and support to other households. The linked page defines unpaid work by the ABS scope: domestic activities, child care, adult care and volunteering. The two pages state no conflicting fact, but a reader following the link may assume the two scopes are the same.
The Usage paragraph gives two 2024 figures: 59.6 hours as the full total, and 55.8 hours with emotional care excluded. The page does explain the difference, but the two numbers sit close together and could be misread as contradicting each other.
