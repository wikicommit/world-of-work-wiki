The HSE source bases it on LFS data averaged over 2009/10-2011/12, and the THOR-GP comparison covers 2013-2015. A reader could take both as current, so the periods are worth stating.
"Men aged 45 and over and women aged 55 and over" is a compact summary. The source lists men 45-54, men 55+ and women 55+ as significantly higher, so the wording is accurate but lumps the male bands together.
