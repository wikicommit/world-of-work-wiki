The EU paragraph lists vibrations from hand tools alongside the other physical factors as associated with MSD complaints. The EU-OSHA report (footnote 136) says this appears to be an indirect relationship that becomes insignificant once the other physical risk factors are controlled for, so a reader may take it as a stronger association than the source shows.
The sentence that workers with MSDs 'more often worked while sick' holds in the source for workers with MSDs and other health problems, compared with workers with no health problems. For workers with only MSDs, the source finds no significant difference from workers with only other health problems. The page does not make this distinction.
