The phrase 'one of two recently added to the joint estimates' describing exposure to long working hours as a risk factor is a bit ambiguous: the source consistently frames the '39 established' and '2 recently added' as pairs of risk factor+health outcome (both recently-added pairs share the same single risk factor, long working hours, paired with stroke and with ischaemic heart disease respectively), not as two separate newly-added risk factors.
