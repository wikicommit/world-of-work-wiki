The opening sentence defines 過労死 itself as "death or serious illness brought on by overwork". The pamphlet never defines 過労死 on its own. It only uses 過労死等 as a label for cerebrovascular and heart diseases and mental disorders caused by overwork. The "serious illness" wording is the page's gloss on that broader term.
The page links "occupational injury" to [[DefinedTerm/occupational-disease]]. That page describes the French maladie professionnelle, as defined in Insee statistics. The Japanese workers' accident compensation (労災) recognition the pamphlet describes is a different system, so a reader could take the link as meaning they are the same concept.
