The report says only that "some courts" have used this criterion, and that other indicators led to different outcomes. Adding "some" would keep the page from implying a uniform trend.

The OSH source also says the duty covers profiling. The page leaves this out, which is fine under the completeness rule, but the Spanish provision reads a little narrower on the page than in the sources.
