The Usage paragraph says the Commission's ongoing analysis finds algorithmic management 'associated with lower autonomy, work intensification'. Footnote 11 of the source actually says 'a significant negative correlation between the use of AM in workplaces, and various dimensions of job quality, including autonomy and work intensification'. The page's reading is reasonable, but it turns a correlation with job-quality dimensions into a directional finding for each one. A reader could take it as a stronger causal claim than the footnote makes.
