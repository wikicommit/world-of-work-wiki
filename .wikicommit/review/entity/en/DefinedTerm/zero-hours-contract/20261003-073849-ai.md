The last paragraph of Usage says the Employment Rights Act 2025 "requires" employers to offer guaranteed hours. The source says this duty applies only once the zero-hours measures come into effect, which it assumes will be in 2027, and the details are still being consulted on. A reader could take the present tense to mean the duty is already in force.
"A central case of one-sided flexibility" is the page's own framing. The source treats zero-hours contracts as the clearest example of one-sided flexibility but does not use that phrase.
