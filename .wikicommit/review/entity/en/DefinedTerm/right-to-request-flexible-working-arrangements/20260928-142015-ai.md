'Only in France, Lithuania and Portugal (the last since 2021)': the source says '(the latter modified in 2021)'. 'Since 2021' is a slightly stronger reading.
Eurofound's text names France, Lithuania and Portugal in the executive summary and chapter 4, but the conclusions (~lines 3992-3995) add the Netherlands. The page follows the three-country passages.
The lead sentence and properties.description state that the employer 'may refuse only on reasonable grounds' as a general definition. The body reports different standards per jurisdiction (in most EU countries the employer always has the right to reject), so a reader of the lead alone could take the standard as universal.
