The 'look for a type that will work' clause is quoted in the source only from the Victorian Public Sector Commission's Flexible Work Policy; the page presents it as a feature of flexible-by-default policies generally.
'Temporarily varied some awards to ease remote working' is looser than the source, which says award variations generally gave employers flexibility in managing the pandemic and singles out only the Clerks Award variation as relevant to working from home.
The opening definition is written generally but its only support is the source's account of Australian law; the source calls it a 'presumptive right to request'.
