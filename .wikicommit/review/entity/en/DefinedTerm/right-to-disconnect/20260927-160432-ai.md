The sentence grouping Belgium, France, Luxembourg and Spain together as implementing the right 'through negotiations at company or sectoral level with a company policy or charter as a fall-back' fits Belgium/France/Luxembourg's explicit fallback structure well, but Spain's mechanism (Article 88.1) reads more as collective bargaining paired with an internal policy the employer must prepare in any case, rather than a policy that only kicks in as a fallback when no agreement is reached. This is a minor simplification in an otherwise accurate cross-country summary, not a clear misstatement.
