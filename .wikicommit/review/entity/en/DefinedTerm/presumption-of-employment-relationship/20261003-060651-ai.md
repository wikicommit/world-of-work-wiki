The page says the EU proposal was "in the version agreed by the Council in June 2023". The ILO report calls it the Council's "General Approach, 7 June 2023", which is the Council's negotiating position and not an adopted directive. "Agreed" could be misread as final adoption, and saying "the Council's general approach of June 2023" would be more precise.
The Croatia bullet says "the facts considered include" a list of indicators. The source also presents that list as the facts considered for the presumption, so the wording is fine, but the list gives no hint of how many indicators must be met. The Belgian and EU bullets state this explicitly.
