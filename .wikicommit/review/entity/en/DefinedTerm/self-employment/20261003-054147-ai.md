The Usage section says the Canadian self-employment rate "has since stayed around 15%". The Statistics Canada article does say the rate fell to 15% in 2001 and "remained relatively stable over the next two decades". But its 2022 figures (11% for women, 16% for men) put the overall rate for 2022 below 15%. A reader could take "around 15%" as a 2022 figure, so the sentence is easy to misread.
The Canada paragraph and the EU paragraph use different definitions of the self-employed. Statistics Canada counts unpaid family workers among the self-employed. Eurostat treats family workers as a category separate from the self-employed. The page states both correctly, but the satisfaction comparison near the end ('contributing family workers' against 'the self-employed') follows the Eurostat grouping, so a reader could carry the Canadian grouping into it.
