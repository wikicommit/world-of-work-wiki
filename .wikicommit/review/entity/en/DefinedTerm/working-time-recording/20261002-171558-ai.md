The Japan paragraph ends by saying that recording working hours is 'the starting point for preventing ill health from overwork'. The pamphlet only says that grasping working hours is 'important' (重要) for preventing health harm from overwork. The phrase 出発点 ('starting point') appears elsewhere in the pamphlet, but about changing the mindset that treats overtime as normal. The page's wording is slightly stronger than the source.
The sentence 'It was issued because improper use of self-reporting had led to unpaid premium wages and excessively long working hours' gives the rationale of the underlying standard on measures employers should take to ascertain working hours properly. The pamphlet only reproduces that standard, so 'it' could be read as the pamphlet itself.
