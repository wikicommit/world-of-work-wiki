The Usage paragraph introduces the napping-procedure list with 'the guidance lists the following'. That is literally accurate (WorkSafe's guidance does list them, source lines 471-479), but in the source WorkSafe presents the list as guidance it reproduces from an external nursing code of practice rather than as its own formulation; a reader may take the items as WorkSafe's own recommendations. WorkSafe's own statements are only line 469 (effective temporary strategy, not routine for extending shifts, needs policy and procedures) and line 452 (provide suitable and safe nap facilities).
