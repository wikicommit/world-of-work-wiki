The page gives WorkSafe NZ's shift-work guidance's National Sleep Foundation figure of 7-9 hours for adults, while the one-hop page DefinedTerm/work-related-fatigue reports WorkSafe's 2017 Fatigue quick guide as saying people generally need 7.5 to 9 hours a night. Each page follows its own source, and the figures belong to different documents, so this is not a contradiction. Still, a reader comparing the two pages could see two different WorkSafe sleep-need figures.
The economic-cost figures ($411 billion, 2.28% of GDP, 1.2 million working days) are framed as 'the same article cites an estimate'. That is accurate, but the estimate comes from a RAND report that the article references and the wiki does not hold.
