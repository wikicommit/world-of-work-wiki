The NZ paragraph says businesses must give night workers training and provide facilities 'Under New Zealand's Health and Safety at Work Act 2015'. The WorkSafe guidance says 'You must provide training and information about the risks of shift work to night workers' and 'you must provide and maintain facilities', and it frames PCBUs' duties under HSWA in general (section 1.1). It never ties these two specific duties to the Act itself, so the attribution is an inference. Wording such as 'WorkSafe's guidance says businesses must…' would follow the source more closely.
The lead sentence gives the Directive's 'night worker' definition with the two criteria joined by 'or'. Article 2(4) as quoted in the Communication joins them with 'and', and the 'or' reading is the Commission's interpretation, which the Usage section explains only later. A reader of the lead alone could take 'or' to be the Directive's own wording.
The '**In statistics.**' paragraph has no blank line before it, so in rendered Markdown it runs on from the WorkSafe paragraph as a single paragraph.
