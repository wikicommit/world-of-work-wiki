The page's Insee definition of underemployment (part-time workers who want more hours and are available, plus people who involuntarily worked less than usual) is narrower than the usage on the linked page DefinedTerm/working-time-mismatch. That page calls anyone who wants more hours than they work 'underemployed', with no availability condition and no short-time working. This is not a factual contradiction, but a reader moving between the two pages could conflate the concepts.
