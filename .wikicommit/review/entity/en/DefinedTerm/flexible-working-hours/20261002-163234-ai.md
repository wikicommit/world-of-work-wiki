The sentence saying earlier studies attribute negative health effects 'mainly to the irregularity of such hours' is easy to misread. In the paper, Giebel et al. and Jansen/Nachreiner blame the irregularity of working time, mentioned right after the temporary extension of hours to accumulate time credit. The page's 'such hours' could be read as the extended hours themselves.
The Related Terms line lists flextime-system, a Japanese statutory arrangement, next to the page's German-sense flextime. The parenthetical flags the Japanese scope, but a reader could still take it as the same concept.
