The WDR 2019 section gives two Peru figures that look inconsistent side by side: informality 'stayed at about 75 percent over the last 30 years' (WDR p. overview, line ~454), and e-Payroll helped cut nonagricultural informal employment 'from 75 percent in 2004 to 68 percent in 2012' (WDR line ~4494). Both are quoted correctly from the source, which itself holds this tension, but a reader may take the two sentences as contradicting each other. Consider saying that both figures come from the same report.
The lead's general definition ('work that takes place outside the social security system and the protections that come with formal employment') is a synthesis over the two sources, not a formulation either one states. It is acceptable because the next sentences say each source's operational definition differs, but it reads as a stand-alone definition.
