In the Family-friendly rights paragraph, the page uses "expands powers" for both the maternity/adoption/shared-parental dismissal-ban provisions, but the source distinguishes them: para 7 says the Act 'amends existing powers' for the maternity/pregnancy/return-to-work ban and separately 'expands existing powers' for Adoption, Shared Parental, Neonatal Care and Bereaved Partners Paternity Leave. The merge is a close paraphrase and not a clear factual error, but a reader comparing closely could be misled about which provision is described as 'amended' versus 'expanded'.
